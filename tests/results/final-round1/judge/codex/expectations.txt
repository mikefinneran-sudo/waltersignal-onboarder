T1: PASS | Defines three ordered, distinctly named tiers: Tier 1 Must, Tier 2 Should, and Tier 3 Could.
T2: PASS | Every tier’s scorecard uses observable actions such as explaining, naming, building, opening, accepting, or adapting.
T3: PASS | Every scorecard line states performance conditions and a numeric or explicit target.
T4: PASS | Each tier has measurable sales-relevant targets due by the day-one 4:00 PM check-in.
T5: PASS | Adjacent tiers have observable anchors: repeat after demonstration, answer after one-line prompt, then perform independently without prompting.
T6: PASS | All Must targets are required; misses receive an example, focused repetition, and reassessment before new material.
T7: PASS | Support decreases explicitly from demonstration and repetition, to one-line prompting, to unprompted independent performance.
