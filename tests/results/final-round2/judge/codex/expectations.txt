T1: PASS | Defines three ordered, distinctly named tiers: Tier 1 Must, Tier 2 Should, and Tier 3 Could.
T2: PASS | Every tier’s scorecard uses observable actions such as explaining, naming, opening, submitting, accepting, or adapting.
T3: PASS | Every scorecard line states performance conditions and an explicit numeric threshold, count, or percentage.
T4: PASS | Every tier has sales-relevant measurable targets due by the day-one 4:00 PM check-in.
T5: PASS | Adjacent tiers are distinguished observably: repeat after demonstration, answer after one prompt, then perform independently without prompting.
T6: PASS | All Must targets are required; misses receive an example, focused repetition, and reassessment at the next check-in before new work.
T7: PASS | Independence explicitly increases from demonstrated repetition, to prompted response, to unprompted solo performance.
