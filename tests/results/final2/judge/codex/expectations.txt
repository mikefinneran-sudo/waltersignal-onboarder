T1: PASS | Defines three ordered, distinctly named tiers: Tier 1 Must, Tier 2 Should, and Tier 3 Could.
T2: PASS | Every tier uses observable outputs such as spoken explanations, submitted drafts, accepted invites, or opened tools.
T3: PASS | Every scorecard row states performance conditions and an explicit numeric threshold, percentage, or complete-part count.
T4: PASS | Each tier has measurable, sales-relevant targets due by the day-one 4:00 PM check-in.
T5: PASS | Adjacent tiers are concretely distinguished by repeating after demonstration, answering one prompt, and performing unprompted, with observable good and weak examples.
T6: PASS | Advancement requires every Must target; misses receive an example, focused repetition, and reassessment at the next daily check-in.
T7: PASS | Tier 1 permits demonstration, Tier 2 permits one prompt, and Tier 3 requires unprompted, no-notes performance.
