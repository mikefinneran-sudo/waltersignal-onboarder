Day 4 is Module 5, The Discovery Call (`reference/sales-process.md`). The folder gives the order and the kinds of questions, not a full script. Start with three:

- **Current state:** How does the work run today? Who calls, what gets written on paper, where does a job fall through the cracks?
- **Gap:** What does that cost them, in their own numbers?
- **Qualifying** (`reference/buyers.md`): Why this? Why now? Why us?

Two more when you are ready: solution and next step.
