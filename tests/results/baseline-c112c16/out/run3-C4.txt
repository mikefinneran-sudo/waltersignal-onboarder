This is module 5, and it's in `reference/sales-process.md`. A discovery call runs in order. Here are the first three parts:

1. **Current state:** How does the work run today? Who calls, what gets written on paper, where does a job fall through the cracks?
2. **Gap:** What does that cost them, in their own numbers?
3. **Solution:** What's the smallest working outcome that closes the gap?

The fourth part, and the three qualifying questions, are next when you're ready.
