Congratulations. Here is what to do today (`reference/sales-process.md`, "After a Yes"):

1. **Tell the Head of Sales today** that the buyer said yes.
2. **Legal & Contracts sends the agreement.** It goes alone, so you don't send it.
3. **Finance & Billing sends the deposit invoice**, but only after the signed agreement comes back. Then the deal moves to Closed Won in the CRM.

Until the signed agreement is back, don't move the deal to Closed Won, and don't mention an invoice or a start date to the buyer. Kickoff belongs to the Delivery Lead. Any note you write to the buyer goes to the Head of Sales first.
