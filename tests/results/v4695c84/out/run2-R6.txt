That is a Delivery Lead question. Delivery Lead owns everything after signing: kickoff, project status, and go-live (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). The folder has no start date, so don't give one.

What to do:

- **Tell the client:** "Good question. I'll get you a straight answer from our Delivery Lead." Don't promise a date. Dates are never yours to give (`reference/the-message.md`).
- **Tell the Head of Sales** about the signing, if you haven't yet.
- **Check with the Head of Sales** before sending anything to the client in writing.

If the client is waiting on the line right now, message the Head of Sales immediately. That is one of the two interrupt cases.

Today the founder holds the Delivery Lead title, but ask by title anyway.
