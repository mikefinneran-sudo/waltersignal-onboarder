A research brief is one page, written before every call (`reference/templates.md`). In module 5 you write three on leads the Head of Sales assigns.

A good one answers three things:
- **Which buyer, and why.** Buyer 1 or 2, with one reason from what you saw.
- **Who decides, who champions.** The owner and the office manager, if public.
- **Why now, plus your first three questions.** A hiring post or a coming season, then three picks from `discovery-questions.md`.

Every claim gets a source link. A filled example is in `templates.md`.

Try it: take one assigned lead and tell me which buyer it is and why.

Also, which modules has the Head of Sales signed off so far, and what have you sold before?
