A brief is one page, written before every call. It tells you which buyer you're talking to and what to ask first. The template and a full example are in `reference/templates.md`.

A good one:
- **Names the buyer type and why.** Example: "1. Owner posts about growth and hiring; this is a workflow question, not a website."
- **Spots a why-now signal.** Example: "Hiring a second dispatcher; heating season starting."
- **Picks your first three questions** from `reference/discovery-questions.md`, and lists every source link.

Try it: pick one of your three assigned leads and write the buyer line and the why-now line. Then tell me why you chose them.

Also, which modules has the Head of Sales signed off so far, and what have you sold before?
