### TURN 3
NEW HIRE: Got it. Who are our clients?
ONBOARDER: From `reference/clients.md`:

**A good-fit client**
- **Size:** an established business of roughly 10 to 250 employees, with revenue coming in and a real operation to improve. Not a startup still finding customers.
- **Situation:** the work runs on spreadsheets, whiteboards, email, and paper, or on several tools that do not share information, so people type the same thing twice.
- **Signs you will hear:** "Quotes take us two days." "Only Sarah knows how that works." "We re-enter every order into the accounting system."
- **Common industries:** field service (HVAC, plumbing, electrical), distribution and wholesale, light manufacturing, multi-location healthcare offices (dental, physical therapy), and professional services firms. No industry is excluded; judge each business on its own facts.

**Who you talk to**

| Role | What they do in the deal |
|---|---|
| **Decider** (owner, CEO, or general manager) | Signs and approves the money. Talk about cost, risk, and what they will be able to see and control. |
| **Champion** (usually the operations or office manager) | Lives with the problem daily. Win them first; the decider listens to them. |
| **Users** (dispatchers, estimators, front-desk staff) | Will use what gets built. Ask to talk to one during discovery. |

**Not a fit** (say so early and kindly, and log the reason in the CRM)
- A website or marketing.
- IT support, computers, or networks.
- A developer by the hour.
- A problem too small for a $20,000 Pilot. Log it as Closed Lost with the reason "too small."

**The four qualifying questions** (ask all four before spending real time on a lead)
1. **Why this?** What problem, in their words?
2. **Why now?** What changed that makes it worth fixing this quarter?
3. **Why us?** What would make WalterSignal the right fit over doing nothing or buying a tool?
4. **Is it big enough?** Would fixing it be worth more to them than a $20,000 Pilot?

If a lead cannot answer "Why now?", log it and set a follow-up date. Do not chase it.

The module 2 milestone is describing all of this and sorting five sample businesses into fit and not a fit. The Head of Sales gives you those five on day 2.

The two client stories you will learn, Brennan Mechanical and Harbor Dental Group, are in `reference/case-study.md`. They are module 3, day 2.
