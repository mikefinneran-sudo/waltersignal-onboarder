Congratulations. Here is what happens next (`reference/sales-process.md`, "After a Yes"):

1. **Today:** tell the Head of Sales at the 4:15 daily check-in (`reference/meetings.md`). Save the call note in the client's Vault folder and log the next step in the CRM with a date, an owner, and what happens.
2. **Legal & Contracts sends the agreement.** You do not send it, and it goes on its own with nothing else attached.
3. **When the signed agreement comes back,** move the deal to **Closed Won** in the CRM, and move the client's Vault folder to match. Until it is signed, the deal stays in its current stage.
4. **Finance & Billing then sends the first invoice.**
5. **The Delivery Lead runs the kickoff.** You stay the client's contact for anything new they want to buy.

What the client pays at signing depends on the phase they said yes to:

| Phase | When the client pays |
|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | 100% at signing |
| Pilot | 50% at signing, 50% at go-live |
| Build | 50% at signing, the balance billed monthly over the Build |
| Support Plan | Monthly, starting the month after go-live |

- **Approval:** if you write the client a thank-you or a "what happens next" note, send the draft to the Head of Sales first (`reference/templates.md`).
- **Promises:** do not give the client a kickoff date or any other date. Timelines come from Solutions Engineering and the Delivery Lead.
- **Not in my notes:** how long the agreement takes to arrive and when kickoff happens. Add both to `reference/question-log.md` for the check-in. Legal & Contracts owns the first, and the Delivery Lead owns the second.
