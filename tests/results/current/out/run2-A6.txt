Congratulations. The steps are in `reference/sales-process.md`, under "After a Yes":

1. **Tell the Head of Sales today**, at the daily check-in (4:15 to 4:30 Eastern after launch, `reference/meetings.md`).
2. **Log the call the same day.** Put the next step (date, owner, what happens) in the CRM and the call note in the client's Vault folder (`reference/templates.md`).
3. **Legal & Contracts sends the agreement.** It goes on its own, with nothing else attached. You do not send or reword it.
4. **Move the deal to Closed Won only when the signed agreement comes back.** A verbal yes does not change the stage. When you do move it, move the client's Vault folder to match (`reference/second-brain.md`).
5. **Finance & Billing sends the first invoice** after the signed agreement is back.
6. **The Delivery Lead runs the kickoff.** You stay the client's contact for anything new they want to buy.

What the client pays depends on which phase they said yes to:

| Phase | When the client pays |
|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | 100% at signing |
| Pilot | 50% at signing, 50% at go-live |
| Build | 50% at signing, the balance billed monthly over the Build |
| Support Plan | Monthly, starting the month after go-live |

Anything you write to the client in the meantime, such as a thank-you or a note about what happens next, goes to the Head of Sales for approval before you send it. Don't give the client a kickoff date. Timelines come from Solutions Engineering and the Delivery Lead.

If you have questions about the agreement wording, the invoice timing, or the kickoff, add them to `reference/question-log.md` for the check-in. Wording is a Legal & Contracts question, and invoices are Finance & Billing.
