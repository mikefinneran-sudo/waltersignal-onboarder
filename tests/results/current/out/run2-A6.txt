Congratulations. Here is what happens next (`reference/sales-process.md`):

1. **Tell the Head of Sales today.** That is your job on a yes.
2. **Legal & Contracts sends the agreement.** It goes alone. You don't send it.
3. **Finance & Billing sends the deposit invoice** only after the signed agreement comes back.

Keep the deal in Proposals until then. It moves to Closed Won when the signed agreement is returned. Log the next step in the CRM today.

Anything you write to the client goes to the Head of Sales first. Don't promise a start date either. After signing, that belongs to the Delivery Lead.

Has the client said yes in writing, or only on a call?
