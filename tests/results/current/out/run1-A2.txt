Here is the 30-second pitch (`reference/the-message.md`):

> WalterSignal builds custom software and AI automation for established businesses whose work has outgrown spreadsheets and disconnected tools. We start by mapping how your work actually runs, prove one fix working on your own numbers, and only then build the rest. Everything we build, you own.

Say it in your own words once you know it, but all four parts must be there:

| Part | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Who it is for** | Established businesses of roughly 10 to 250 people, running on spreadsheets, whiteboards, and tools that do not talk to each other |
| **Understand first** | We map how the work runs before recommending anything |
| **Prove before building** | One workflow goes live and is measured before the full build |
| **You own it** | The client owns the code, the data, and the accounts |

**Never say:**
- A delivery date. Dates come from Solutions Engineering after scoping.
- A promised result (hours saved, revenue gained) for a prospect's business. Case study results belong to those clients.
- A discount, or a hint of one. Discounts belong to the Founder & CEO.
- Technology first ("AI agents," "LLMs," tool names). Lead with the client's work.
- That WalterSignal sells a product or a software subscription. It does client work only.
