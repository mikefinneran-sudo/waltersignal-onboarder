On day 12 you are in Tier 3 (days 11 to 30, "in production, done together"). The Head of Sales checks the scorecard below (`reference/expectations.md`). It applies once the Head of Sales has said "launched" at the Launch Gate check (`reference/syllabus.md`).

**What you should be able to do**
- Run live discovery calls with the Head of Sales on the line.
- Quote the Quotable prices in `reference/services.md` when a client asks.
- Draft follow-ups for approval.
- Present your own deals at the Monday pipeline review (`reference/meetings.md`): the stage, the next step, and its date for each deal.
- Complete Continuing Education CE1, The Discovery Sprint in Depth (weeks 3 to 4).

**Scorecard**

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Calls ending with a next step logged the same day | 100% |
| Research brief written before each call | 100% |
| Call notes saved in the Vault the same day | 100% |
| Anything sent to a client without approval | 0 |
| CE1 signed off | By day 30 |

You bring your numbers against these targets to the Friday 1:1, in the 5 to 15 minute block (`reference/meetings.md`).

**Not expected yet**
- Proposals (CE4, weeks 9 to 10).
- Scoping a Pilot (CE2, weeks 5 to 6).
- Internal prices and discounts. Build prices and discounts are never yours; they belong to the Founder & CEO.

**Not in my notes:** meeting, pipeline, and revenue targets. The Head of Sales sets those at launch, and they are not in this folder. The same goes for your quota and commission plan. If you have not been given them, add them to `reference/question-log.md` for the daily check-in.
