Tell them the first step is a **Discovery Sprint at a fixed $7,500**, and yes, it counts toward the next phase (`reference/services.md`).

**What you can say:**

- **Price:** $7,500, fixed. It is a Quotable price, so you can say it now that you are launched.
- **What they get:** 2 weeks of interviews with their team, a map of how the work runs today, a ranked list of what to fix and what to leave alone, a recommended Pilot, and a 60-minute readout with the decider.
- **The credit:** if they sign a Pilot within 30 days of the Sprint readout, the full $7,500 is credited toward it.
- **If they ask what the Pilot costs:** $20,000 for one workflow connected to up to two existing tools, or $30,000 for three or more tools or an AI step. Both are Quotable.
- **Payment:** the Sprint is 100% at signing (`reference/sales-process.md`).

**Wording from `reference/objections.md`:**

> "The first step is a two-week Discovery Sprint at a fixed $7,500, and it's credited toward the Pilot if you go ahead within 30 days."

For proof, Brennan Mechanical paid $7,500 for the Sprint, then $30,000 for the Pilot less the $7,500 credit (`reference/case-study.md`). Tell it as Brennan's story, not as a promise of what this prospect will pay.

**Do not:**

- Quote a Build price or a range. The Founder & CEO prices every Build.
- Offer or hint at a discount. If they push on price, say "Let me take that to our Founder."

If you put any of this in writing, send the draft to the Head of Sales for approval first.
