Quotable means you may say it to a client after the Launch Gate. Internal means it is only for qualifying a lead and is never said to a client (`reference/services.md`).

**Until the Launch Gate at the end of day 10, you quote no price to anyone, even the Quotable ones.** If a prospect asks for a price before then, bring in the Head of Sales (`reference/objections.md`).

| Item | Price | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint (2 weeks) | $7,500 fixed | Quotable |
| Pilot, one workflow connected to up to two existing tools (6 to 8 weeks) | $20,000 | Quotable |
| Pilot, three or more tools or includes an AI step | $30,000 | Quotable |
| Support Plan, Essentials | $1,500/month | Quotable |
| Support Plan, Standard | $3,000/month | Quotable |
| Support Plan, Priority | $5,000/month | Quotable |
| Build (3 to 6 months) | Usually $60,000 to $180,000, priced per module from the Pilot | Internal. The Founder & CEO prices every Build. |
| Change request | $175/hour | Internal. The Delivery Lead sends the written quote, not you. |

- **Sprint credit:** if the client signs a Pilot within 30 days of the Sprint readout, the full $7,500 is credited toward the Pilot.
- **Support Plans:** they are month to month. Essentials includes hosting, monitoring, and fixes, with a 2-business-day response. Standard adds up to 10 hours of changes a month, with a 1-business-day response. Priority adds up to 25 hours a month, with a same-day response.
- **Discounts:** you never offer, hint at, or agree to one. If a client asks "Can you do it cheaper?", say "Let me take that to our Founder," and route it to the Founder & CEO.
- **Anything not on the price list:** don't guess. The Founder & CEO prices anything not in `services.md`.

Your module 6 milestone is to name every Quotable and Internal price and say which is which. Today's goals are `reference/objections.md` and a five-objection role-play with the Head of Sales.
