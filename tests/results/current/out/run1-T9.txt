That isn't in my notes, so I can't tell you yes or no. Whether we can build on Salesforce is a technical scope question, and **Solutions Engineering** owns it (`reference/who-owns-what.md`).

- **Log it:** Add it to `reference/question-log.md` for the daily check-in, with the prospect's name and what they want to do on Salesforce. Solutions Engineering will need that detail to answer.
- **Holding line:** Don't say yes or no to the prospect. Something like: "Good question. I'll get you a straight answer from our Solutions Engineering lead." That's the pattern in `reference/objections.md`.
- **No promises:** Don't promise a date or a result along with it (`reference/the-message.md`, "Never Say This").
- **Approval:** Anything you send the prospect about this goes to the Head of Sales first.
- **Urgent case:** If the prospect is on the line right now, that is one of the two cases where you message the Head of Sales in Slack immediately.
