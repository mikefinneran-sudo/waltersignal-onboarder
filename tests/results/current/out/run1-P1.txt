WalterSignal is a 14-person software and AI consultancy. It does client work only: custom operations software and AI automation for established businesses of roughly 10 to 250 employees that have outgrown spreadsheets and disconnected tools. It sells no products or subscriptions. You are the first salesperson it has hired, and the team is remote-first on US Eastern time. Your first 10 days are training, and you start live selling at the Launch Gate on day 10.

## Today's goals (`reference/first-ten-days.md`)

1. **Welcome call** with the Head of Sales at 9:00 AM Eastern. Expect to talk about what you have sold before, to whom, and what you want to learn.
2. **Accounts:** Revenue Operations sets up every tool in `reference/tools.md` after the call.
3. **Module 1, the pitch:** read `reference/the-message.md`.
4. **Start module 2, our clients:** read `reference/clients.md`.
5. **Daily check-in** with the Head of Sales, 4:00 to 4:30 PM Eastern (`reference/meetings.md`).

**Today is done when:**
- Every account in `tools.md` works.
- You can say the 30-second pitch at the check-in.
- Your question list is started.

The full Day 1 checklist adds two items: you can describe a good-fit client and the decider and champion inside one, and you know that anything sent to a client goes to the Head of Sales first.

**Not expected today** (`reference/expectations.md`): pitching anyone outside the company, quoting any price, contacting any prospect, or sending anything.

## Rules to keep from the start

- **No prices to anyone before the Launch Gate**, including friends and family.
- **Anything for a client or prospect goes to the Head of Sales first**, at every tier.
- **Questions I can't answer:** add them to `reference/question-log.md` for the daily check-in. Interrupt someone only if a client is waiting on the line, or something went to a client by mistake. In either case, message the Head of Sales in Slack.
