Today is about learning the ground. The rest of the job is paced over 90 days, so here is today in full, then the map.

## What WalterSignal is
A 14-person software and AI consultancy that does client work only. It builds custom operations software and AI automation for established businesses of roughly 10 to 250 employees. It sells no products or subscriptions. The team is remote-first on US Eastern time. You are the first salesperson hired; until now the Founder & CEO and the Head of Sales did all the selling.

## Today's goals (`reference/first-ten-days.md`)
1. **Welcome call** with the Head of Sales at 9:00 AM Eastern. You cover what you have sold before, to whom, and what you want to learn.
2. **Accounts:** Revenue Operations sets up every tool in `reference/tools.md`.
3. **Module 1, the pitch:** read `reference/the-message.md`.
4. **Start module 2, our clients:** read `reference/clients.md`.

**The day is done when:**
- Every account in `reference/tools.md` works.
- You can say the 30-second pitch at the 4:00 PM check-in.
- Your question list is started (`reference/question-log.md`).

The check-in runs 4:00 to 4:30 daily through day 10 (`reference/meetings.md`).

## Not expected today (`reference/expectations.md`)
- Pitching to anyone outside the company
- Quoting any price
- Contacting any prospect
- Sending anything to anyone

Anything that goes to a client or prospect is approved by the Head of Sales first, at every tier.
