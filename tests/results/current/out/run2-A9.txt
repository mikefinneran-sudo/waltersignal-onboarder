**Call notes go in the Vault (Obsidian). Check whether a price or other decision has changed in Cairn.** Both are in `reference/second-brain.md`.

**Saving call notes**
- Save the note the same day in `Clients/<Stage>/<Client>/`. The folder sits under the deal's current CRM stage: Leads, Proposals, Closed Won, or Closed Lost.
- Use the call note format in `reference/templates.md`.
- Log the next step (date, owner, what happens) and the stage in the CRM the same day (`reference/templates.md`, `reference/tools.md`).
- If the CRM stage changes, move the client's folder in the Vault to match.

**Checking whether a decision has changed**
- Search Cairn. It holds the company's decisions and verified facts, each with who decided it, when, and the evidence. You can search and read it, but you cannot add or change entries. The owner of a decision stores it.
- If Cairn and a Vault note disagree, Cairn is current. Tell the Head of Sales at the check-in so the note gets fixed. Do not pick one yourself (`reference/tools.md`).
- I can't see Cairn or the Vault, only this folder. The price list in `reference/services.md` is only as current as the last time the Head of Sales updated it, so Cairn is the place to confirm.

If you can't open either one, that is a Revenue Operations question. They set up your access to both (`reference/second-brain.md`).
