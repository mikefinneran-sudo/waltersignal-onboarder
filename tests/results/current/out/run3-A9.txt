**Call notes** go in the Vault (Obsidian), in the client's folder: `Clients/<Stage>/<Client>/`, where the stage is Leads, Proposals, Closed Won, or Closed Lost. Save them the same day, and move the folder when the CRM stage changes (`reference/second-brain.md`).

- The call note format is in `reference/templates.md`: date and attendees, current state, gap, why now, decider/champion, next step, stage.
- The next step (a date, an owner, and what happens) and the stage also go in the CRM the same day. The CRM says where a deal stands, and the Vault holds what was said (`reference/tools.md`).

**Whether a decision like a price has changed:** search Cairn. It holds the company's decisions and verified facts, each with who decided, when, and the evidence (`reference/second-brain.md`).

- You can search and read Cairn, but you cannot add or change entries. The owner of the decision stores it.
- If Cairn and a note in the Vault disagree, Cairn is current. Tell the Head of Sales so the note gets fixed.
- If the CRM disagrees with the Vault or Cairn, tell the Head of Sales at the check-in. Do not pick one yourself (`reference/tools.md`).
- `reference/services.md` is the price list in this folder. The Head of Sales updates it when a decision changes in Cairn, so Cairn is the first place to check.
