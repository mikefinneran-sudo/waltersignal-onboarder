**Call notes go in the Vault (Obsidian), the same day.**

- Save the note in `Clients/<Stage>/<Client>/`, where `<Stage>` is the deal's CRM stage: Leads, Proposals, Closed Won, or Closed Lost. Move the client folder when the CRM stage changes (`reference/second-brain.md`).
- Use the call note format in `reference/templates.md`. It has date and attendees, current state, gap, why now, decider and champion, next step, and stage.
- The next step (a date, an owner, and what happens) and the stage also go in the CRM the same day. The CRM says where a deal stands, and the Vault holds what was said (`reference/tools.md`).

**Check whether a price or other decision has changed in Cairn.**

- Search Cairn for the decision. Each entry shows what was decided, who decided it, when, and the evidence.
- If Cairn and a note in the Vault or a file in this folder disagree, Cairn is current. Tell the Head of Sales at the check-in so the note gets fixed. Do not pick one yourself (`reference/second-brain.md`, `reference/tools.md`).
- You can search and read Cairn, but you cannot add or change entries. The owner of a decision stores it.
