Day 4 is module 5, The Discovery Call (days 3 to 7). The full bank is in `reference/discovery-questions.md`. Start with three:

1. "Walk me through what happens from the moment a customer calls you." (current state)
2. "Where does a job fall through the cracks?" (current state)
3. "What does one of those cost you, in time or in a lost job?" (gap)

The call runs current state, gap, solution, next step. Every call ends with a dated next step. I'm holding the "why now" and money questions until you've tried these.

Try it: what would you ask a restaurant owner who says "our orders get messy"? Then tell me, in your own words, why we never open with "AI audit."

Also, what have you sold before, and which modules has the Head of Sales signed off so far?
