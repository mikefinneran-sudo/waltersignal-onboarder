No. You never offer, hint at, or agree to a discount, at any tier. Only the Founder & CEO decides discounts (`reference/services.md`, `reference/who-owns-what.md`). That holds on day 40 (`reference/expectations.md` lists discounts as not yours through day 90).

What to do:

- **If the client asks for a lower price:** say "Let me take that to our Founder." Don't suggest a number or hint that one is possible (`reference/objections.md`, "Can You Do It Cheaper?").
- **Get the question to the owner:** add it to `reference/question-log.md` and bring it to today's daily check-in with the Head of Sales. Messaging the Head of Sales in Slack right away is only for two cases: a client is waiting on the line, or something went to a client by mistake. A deal you'd like to close this week is not one of them.
- **Before anything goes to the client:** the Head of Sales approves it first, including any follow-up about price.

The price list in `reference/services.md` is the only pricing you can use. The Founder & CEO prices every Build. Whether a discount could be approved, and how fast, isn't in my notes, so I can't say.
