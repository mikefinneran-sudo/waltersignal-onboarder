**Legal & Contracts** handles it. Agreement wording and changes to terms are theirs (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). Contract terms are also still outside what you do at your stage (`reference/expectations.md`).

What to do:

1. **Don't agree to or hint at any change** to the client. Wording is not yours to answer.
2. **Tell the Head of Sales** at the daily check-in. Anything that goes back to the client is approved by the Head of Sales first.
3. **Add it to `reference/question-log.md`** for the check-in, noting which clause and what the client wants changed.
4. **Interrupt only in the two urgent cases:** if the client is waiting on the line, or something went to the client by mistake. Then message the Head of Sales in Slack right away.

If the change affects price or a discount, that belongs to the Founder & CEO (`reference/who-owns-what.md`).
