**Legal & Contracts** handles it. Agreement wording, changes to terms, NDAs, and sending the agreement all belong to that title, and "The client wants to change clause 4" is the example in `reference/who-owns-what.md`.

What you do:

- **Do not** comment on the wording or promise the client any change. Contract wording is outside what you answer.
- **Tell the client** you will take the request to the right person. Anything that goes to the client in writing is approved by the Head of Sales first.
- **Tell the Head of Sales** at the daily check-in, and keep the deal's CRM stage and the client's Vault folder current.

This is not one of the two urgent cases (a client waiting on the line, or something sent to a client by mistake), so it does not need an interrupt. If the client is on the line right now, message the Head of Sales in Slack.
