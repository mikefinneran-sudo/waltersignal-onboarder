**A Build usually takes 3 to 6 months.** That is the range in `reference/services.md`, and `reference/objections.md` repeats it for the "How long will it take?" objection. A Build is scoped after the Pilot, and it is delivered in modules, with documentation and staff training.

The exact length for this 20-person HVAC company isn't in my notes, because it depends on the modules the Pilot points to. Timelines before signing belong to Solutions Engineering.

- **Say to the prospect:** "A Build usually runs 3 to 6 months, and Solutions Engineering gives you a firm timeline once we've scoped it after the Pilot."
- **Don't say:** a specific date or duration for their business. Never promise a delivery date (`reference/the-message.md`).
- **Closest comparison:** Brennan Mechanical, a 38-person HVAC contractor, had a 5-month Build covering a dispatch board, a technician phone app, and an automatic hand-off to accounting (`reference/case-study.md`). Tell it as Brennan's story, not as a forecast for this prospect.
- **Price:** don't quote or hint at a Build price. Build prices are Internal, and the Founder & CEO prices every Build (`reference/services.md`).

If you need a timeline for this specific company, add it to `reference/question-log.md` for Solutions Engineering. Anything you send the prospect about timing goes to the Head of Sales for approval first.
