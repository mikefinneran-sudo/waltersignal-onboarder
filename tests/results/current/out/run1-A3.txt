Tell them the first step is a Discovery Sprint at a fixed **$7,500**, and yes, it counts toward the next phase (`reference/services.md`).

**What to say**
- **The Sprint:** two weeks. The client gets interviews with their team, a map of how the work runs today, a ranked list of what to fix and what to leave alone, a recommended Pilot, and a 60-minute readout with the decider.
- **The credit:** if they sign a Pilot within 30 days of the Sprint readout, the full $7,500 is credited toward the Pilot.
- **The Pilot:** $20,000 for one workflow connected to up to two existing tools, or $30,000 if it connects to three or more tools or includes an AI step. Both are Quotable after launch.
- **Payment:** the Sprint is 100% at signing (`reference/sales-process.md`).

**What not to say**
- Don't quote a Build price or range. The Founder & CEO prices every Build.
- Don't offer or hint at a discount. Discounts belong to the Founder & CEO.
- Don't promise the Sprint will lead to a Pilot or to any result. A client can stop after any phase and keep what was delivered.

**Proof:** if they ask for an example, Brennan Mechanical paid the $7,500 Sprint and had it credited to their Pilot (`reference/case-study.md`).

If you send a proposal or a follow-up, the Head of Sales approves the draft first. The Sprint proposal is one page at the fixed price.
