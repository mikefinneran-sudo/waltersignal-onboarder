Yes, you can quote this now that you're launched. The first step is the **Discovery Sprint**, and it does count toward the next phase (`reference/services.md`).

**What to tell them:**

- **Price:** $7,500, fixed.
- **Length:** 2 weeks.
- **What they get:** interviews with their team, a map of how the work runs today, a ranked list of what to fix and what to leave alone, a recommended Pilot, and a 60-minute readout with the decider.
- **Credit:** if they sign a Pilot within 30 days of the Sprint readout, the full $7,500 is credited toward the Pilot price.

A line you can use (from `reference/objections.md`): "The first step is a two-week Discovery Sprint at a fixed $7,500, and it's credited toward the Pilot if you go ahead within 30 days."

**Keep in mind:**

- The credit applies only to a Pilot signed within the 30 days. Don't say it carries over after that.
- Don't promise they will go ahead with a Pilot or quote them a Pilot or Build number unless they ask. If they ask about Build cost, the Founder & CEO prices every Build, so you don't give a range.
- The Sprint is paid 100% at signing (`reference/sales-process.md`).
- If you send any of this in writing, the Head of Sales approves the draft first.
