## Today (Day 1, `reference/first-ten-days.md`)

1. **Welcome call** with the Head of Sales at 9:00 AM Eastern, on Google Meet. Expect to talk about what you have sold before, to whom, and what you want to learn.

**The day is done when:**
- Every account works.
- You can say the 30-second pitch without notes at the 4:00 PM Eastern check-in.
- Your question list is started and was reviewed at that check-in. Unanswered questions go in `reference/question-log.md`.

**Also today:** you can describe a good-fit client, and the decider and champion inside one. Send nothing to anyone. Anything that goes to a client or prospect is approved by the Head of Sales first, at every tier.
