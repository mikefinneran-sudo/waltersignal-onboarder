## What WalterSignal Is

WalterSignal is a 14-person software and AI consultancy. It does client work only: custom operations software and AI automation for established businesses of roughly 10 to 250 employees. It sells no products and no subscriptions. You are its first salesperson, and the team is remote-first on US Eastern time (`reference/first-ten-days.md`).

## Today's Goals (`reference/first-ten-days.md`)

1. **Welcome call** with the Head of Sales at 9:00 AM Eastern. They will ask what you have sold before, to whom, and what you want to learn.
2. **Accounts:** Revenue Operations sets up every tool in `reference/tools.md`.
3. **Module 1, the pitch:** read `reference/the-message.md`, then start module 2 by reading `reference/clients.md`.

**Day 1 is done when:**
- Every account in `reference/tools.md` works.
- You can say the 30-second pitch at the 4:00 PM check-in.
- Your question list is started (`reference/question-log.md`) and was reviewed at that check-in.
- You can describe a good-fit client and the decider and champion inside one.
- You know that anything going to a client goes to the Head of Sales first.

**Not expected today** (`reference/expectations.md`): pitching anyone outside the company, quoting any price, contacting any prospect, or sending anything.
