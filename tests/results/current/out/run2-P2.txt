Proposals are not expected of you yet. Writing them is Continuing Education CE4 (weeks 9 to 10), and the milestone is a Good, Better, Best proposal for a real deal (`reference/syllabus.md`). Here is how they work.

**What a proposal is:** the written offer for the client's next phase (`reference/sales-process.md`).

| Type | Format |
|---|---|
| **Discovery Sprint** | One page, at the fixed $7,500 price. Your first sale with a new client is almost always this one. |
| **Pilot and Build** | Three options: Good, Better, Best. Each step up adds real scope (another workflow, another connected tool, or a Support Plan). Best is the one you recommend. |

**Rules for every proposal:**
- Write it in the client's words, not technical language.
- Pilot prices come from `reference/services.md`. The Founder & CEO prices every Build, so you draft and they price.
- The Head of Sales approves every proposal before it is sent. Once approved, it goes to the client as a Google Doc (`reference/tools.md`).

**After it is sent:**
- The deal moves from Leads to Proposals in the CRM.
- It moves to Closed Won when the signed agreement comes back, or to Closed Lost if the client says no or goes quiet.
- On a yes, tell the Head of Sales the same day. Legal & Contracts sends the agreement, and Finance & Billing sends the first invoice once it is signed.

A proposal template or a filled example is not in my notes. `reference/templates.md` covers only the research brief, call note, and follow-up email. Add it to `reference/question-log.md` for the Head of Sales at the check-in.

You quote no price to anyone until the Launch Gate on day 10.

Today's goals are the welcome call, your accounts, and module 1, the pitch (`reference/first-ten-days.md`).
