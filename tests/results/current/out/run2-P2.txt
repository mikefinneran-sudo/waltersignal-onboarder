Proposals are Continuing Education CE4 (weeks 9 to 10), so nothing is expected of you on this yet. Here is what the folder says (`reference/sales-process.md`, `reference/syllabus.md`).

**What a proposal is:** the written offer for the next phase.

| Phase | Proposal format |
|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | One page, at the fixed $7,500 price |
| Pilot and Build | Three options: Good, Better, Best |

**Rules for all proposals:**
- **Good, Better, Best:** each step up adds real scope, such as another workflow, another connected tool, or a Support Plan. Best is the option you recommend.
- **Language:** write it in the client's words, not technical language.
- **Pricing:** Pilot prices come from `reference/services.md`. The Founder & CEO prices every Build.
- **Approval:** the Head of Sales approves every proposal before it is sent.
- **After sending:** the deal moves from Leads to Proposals in the CRM.

**When you will learn it:** the CE4 milestone is to draft a Good, Better, Best proposal for a real deal, for the Head of Sales to approve and the Founder & CEO to price. Your tier-4 scorecard (`reference/expectations.md`) asks for at least one proposal drafted by day 90.

**What isn't in my notes:** there is no proposal template in `reference/templates.md`, so I can't give you a layout or section list. If you want one sooner, add it to `reference/question-log.md` for the Head of Sales at the 4:00 check-in.

Today is about the pitch (module 1) and good-fit clients (module 2), plus getting your accounts working. You quote no price to anyone until the Launch Gate on day 10.
