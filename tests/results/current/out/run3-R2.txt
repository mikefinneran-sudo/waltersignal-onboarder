Revenue Operations owns the CRM, tool access, and logins (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). `reference/first-ten-days.md` says the same for any account that doesn't work.

- **Contact them by title in Slack.** Everyone is in Slack under their title.
- **Don't treat it as urgent.** Only two cases justify interrupting the Head of Sales: a client waiting on the line, or something sent to a client by mistake. A login problem is neither.
- **Raise it at the 4:00 PM Eastern check-in** if it isn't fixed by then. Every account working was a Day 1 goal, so the Head of Sales should know. Add it to your question list (`reference/question-log.md`).

The CRM is a tool you need for module 4 on Day 3, when Revenue Operations walks you through it, so it's worth getting fixed today.
