## The job

WalterSignal is a 14-person, remote-first consultancy that works on US Eastern time. It does client work only: custom operations software and AI automation for established businesses of roughly 10 to 250 employees. It sells no products or subscriptions. You are its first salesperson; until now the Founder & CEO and the Head of Sales did all the selling.

## Today's goals

1. **Welcome call** with the Head of Sales at 9:00 AM Eastern. Expect to talk about what you have sold before, to whom, and what you want to learn.
2. **Accounts:** Revenue Operations sets up every tool in `reference/tools.md` after the welcome call.
3. **Module 1, the pitch:** read `reference/the-message.md`.
4. **Start module 2, our clients:** read `reference/clients.md`.
5. **Daily check-in** with the Head of Sales, 4:00 to 4:30 PM Eastern (`reference/meetings.md`). Bring your question list.

**Today is done when:**
- Every account in `tools.md` works.
- You can say the 30-second pitch without notes.
- You can describe a good-fit client, and the decider and champion inside one.
- Your question list is started and was reviewed at the check-in.

The fifth checklist item is that you know anything sent to a client goes to the Head of Sales first.

## Not expected today

You are not expected to pitch anyone outside the company, quote a price, contact a prospect, or send anything (`reference/expectations.md`).

## Two rules from day one

- **Approval:** the Head of Sales approves anything that goes to a client or prospect, at every stage.
- **Prices:** you quote no price to anyone until you pass the Launch Gate on day 10, including friends and family.

## Questions

Questions I cannot answer go in `reference/question-log.md` for the daily check-in. Interrupt the Head of Sales in Slack only if a client is waiting on the line, or something went to a client by mistake (`reference/who-owns-what.md`).

**Initial Training, days 1 to 10** (`reference/syllabus.md`):

| # | Module | When |

Each module ends with a milestone that only the Head of Sales signs off. The **Launch Gate** is at the end of day 10, and then you start live selling.

**Continuing Education, days 11 to 90:** CE1 to CE5, about one topic every two weeks, ending in your first solo discovery calls. The 90-day review closes it out.

Other recurring meetings start later: the Friday 1:1 on day 5, and the Monday pipeline review on day 6.
