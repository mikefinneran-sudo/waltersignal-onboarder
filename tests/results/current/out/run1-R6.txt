The **Delivery Lead** answers. Kickoff and everything after signing belong to that title (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). "When does a signed client's project start?" is one of the example questions listed under it.

What you do:

- **Don't give a date yourself.** Dates and timelines are never yours to promise (`reference/the-message.md`). Tell the client you will get them the answer from the Delivery Lead.
- **Check the CRM stage.** Once the signed agreement is back, the deal should be in Closed Won. Finance & Billing then sends the first invoice (`reference/sales-process.md`).
- **Tell the Head of Sales the same day,** at the daily check-in (`reference/sales-process.md`).
- **Get approval before anything goes to the client.** The Head of Sales approves every message first, including a reply about the start date.

You stay the client's contact for anything new they want to buy. The Delivery Lead runs the kickoff.

If the client is waiting on the line right now, that is one of the two cases where you message the Head of Sales in Slack immediately (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). Otherwise, add it to your question list for the check-in.
