**The Delivery Lead answers.** The Delivery Lead owns everything after signing, including kickoff and when the project starts (`reference/who-owns-what.md`, `reference/sales-process.md`).

What to do:

1. **Give the client no date.** The folder has no start date, and you never promise one (`reference/the-message.md`). You can tell them the Delivery Lead runs the kickoff.
2. **Add the question to `reference/question-log.md`** and bring it to the daily check-in. If the client is on the line right now, message the Head of Sales in Slack right away. That is one of the two cases that justify interrupting.
3. **Tell the Head of Sales about the signing** at the daily check-in if you haven't yet (`reference/sales-process.md`).
4. **Get the Head of Sales's approval** before you send the client anything in writing.

You remain the client's contact for anything new they want to buy (`reference/sales-process.md`).
