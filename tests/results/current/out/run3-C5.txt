Day 5 is module 5, where you write three research briefs on leads the Head of Sales assigns. The template and a filled example are in `reference/templates.md`.

A good brief is one page, written before the call. Three things make it good:

- **Which buyer, and why.** Buyer 1 or 2, with one sentence of evidence from their site or job posts.
- **Likely champion.** Usually the office manager, if their name is public.
- **Your first three questions.** Pick them from `reference/discovery-questions.md`.

List every link you used under Sources.

Try it: draft one on your first assigned lead, then tell me in your own words why "which buyer" comes first.

Also, which modules has the Head of Sales signed off so far, and what have you sold before?
