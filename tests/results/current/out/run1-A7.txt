Bring four things, in the order the meeting runs (`reference/meetings.md`). The weekly 1:1 is Fridays, 2:00 to 2:30 Eastern, and it is your meeting: you talk first.

| Minutes | Item | What you bring |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | One win and one thing that was hard this week | Both, in a sentence each |
| 5 to 15 | Your scorecard for your tier | Your numbers against each target (below) |
| 15 to 25 | One skill to practice next week | A call or draft you want coached |
| 25 to 30 | Anything not on the question list | Whatever is on your mind |

**Your scorecard** is Tier 3, days 11 to 30 (`reference/expectations.md`). Have your own number ready for each line:

| Scorecard line | Target |
|---|---|
| Calls ending with a next step logged the same day | 100% |
| Research brief written before each call | 100% |
| Call notes saved in the Vault the same day | 100% |
| Anything sent to a client without approval | 0 |
| CE1 (The Discovery Sprint in Depth) signed off | By day 30 |

CE1 is scheduled for weeks 3 to 4 (`reference/syllabus.md`). Say where you stand on it, but you are not expected to have it signed off yet.

For the coaching slot, pick a real call or a follow-up draft from this week. Templates for both are in `reference/templates.md`.

Anything you could not get answered from this folder goes in `reference/question-log.md`. Bring it to the daily check-in, not the 1:1.
