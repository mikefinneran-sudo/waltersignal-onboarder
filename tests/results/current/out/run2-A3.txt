Tell them the first step is a Discovery Sprint at a fixed **$7,500**, and yes, it counts toward the next phase (`reference/services.md`).

**What to say**
- "The first step is a two-week Discovery Sprint at a fixed $7,500. If you go ahead with a Pilot within 30 days of the Sprint readout, the full $7,500 is credited toward it." (`reference/objections.md` has the same wording.)

**Details if they ask**
- **What they get:** interviews with the team, a map of how the work runs today, a ranked list of what to fix and what to leave alone, a recommended Pilot, and a 60-minute readout with the decider.
- **Credit:** it applies only to a Pilot signed within 30 days of the readout.
- **Payment:** the Sprint is 100% at signing (`reference/sales-process.md`).
- **Stopping:** each phase is signed on its own, so they can stop after the Sprint and keep what was delivered.

**Do not say**
- Any promise of a result or a date.
- Any hint of a discount. Only the Founder & CEO decides those.
- A Build price or range. Builds are Internal and priced by the Founder & CEO.

If you put any of this in writing, send the draft to the Head of Sales for approval first.
