- A map of how the work runs today

- A 60-minute readout with the decider

**If they ask what the credit goes toward:**
- A Pilot is **$20,000** for one workflow connected to up to two existing tools.
- It is **$30,000** if it connects to three or more tools or includes an AI step.

**Other points:**
- Each phase is sold and signed on its own, so they can stop after the Sprint and keep what was delivered.
- The Sprint is paid 100% at signing (`reference/sales-process.md`).
