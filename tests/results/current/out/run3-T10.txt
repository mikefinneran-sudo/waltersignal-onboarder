A Build usually takes 3 to 6 months (`reference/services.md`). The folder has nothing that narrows that range for a 20-person HVAC company, because a Build is scoped after the Pilot, from what the Pilot shows. Any estimate beyond the range would be a guess.

- **Closest example:** Brennan Mechanical, a 38-person HVAC contractor, had a 5-month Build in three modules (`reference/case-study.md`). That is their story, not a forecast for another client.
- **What you can say to a prospect:** the Sprint takes 2 weeks, a Pilot 6 to 8 weeks, and a Build is scoped after the Pilot, usually 3 to 6 months (`reference/objections.md`, "How Long Will It Take?").
- **What you must not say:** a delivery date or a firm Build length. Dates come from Solutions Engineering after scoping (`reference/the-message.md`).

If you need a specific timeline for a live deal, that is a Solutions Engineering question (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). Add it to `reference/question-log.md` for the daily check-in.
