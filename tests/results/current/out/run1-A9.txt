**Call notes go in the Vault. Price and other decision changes are checked in Cairn.**

**Saving call notes** (`reference/second-brain.md`, `reference/templates.md`, `reference/tools.md`)
- Save the note in the Vault (Obsidian), in `Clients/<Stage>/<Client>/`, the same day. The stage is the client's CRM stage: Leads, Proposals, Closed Won, or Closed Lost.
- Use the call note template in `templates.md`: date and attendees, current state, gap, why now, decider and champion, next step, and stage.
- Put the next step and the stage in the CRM the same day too. The CRM says where a deal stands, and the Vault holds what was said.
- When the CRM stage changes, move the client's folder to match.

**Checking whether a decision changed** (`reference/second-brain.md`)
- Search Cairn. It holds the company's decisions and verified facts, each with who decided it, when, and the evidence.
- If Cairn and a Vault note disagree, Cairn is current. Tell the Head of Sales so the note gets fixed.
- If the CRM, Vault, and Cairn disagree with each other, tell the Head of Sales at the check-in. Don't pick one yourself.
- You can search and read Cairn, but you can't add or change entries. The owner of a decision stores it.
