Call notes go in the Vault, and Cairn is where you check whether a decision has changed (`reference/second-brain.md`).

**Saving a call note**
- **Note:** save it the same day in the Vault (Obsidian), in `Clients/<Stage>/<Client>/`. The stage is the deal's CRM stage: Leads, Proposals, Closed Won, or Closed Lost.
- **CRM:** log the next step (a date, an owner, and what happens) and the stage in the CRM the same day (`reference/tools.md`).
- **Format:** the call note template is in `reference/templates.md`.
- **Stage changes:** when the CRM stage changes, move the client's folder in the Vault to match.

**Checking whether a decision has changed**
- Search Cairn, the company's searchable store of decisions and verified facts. Each entry shows what was decided, who decided it, when, and the evidence.
- You can search and read Cairn, but you cannot add or change entries. The owner of a decision stores it.
- If Cairn and a Vault note disagree, Cairn is current. Tell the Head of Sales at the check-in so the note gets fixed. Don't pick one yourself.
