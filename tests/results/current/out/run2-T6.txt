No. You never offer, hint at, or agree to a discount, at any tier. Only the Founder & CEO decides discounts (`reference/services.md`, `reference/who-owns-what.md`). On day 40 you are in Tier 4, and discounts are still on the "not yours" list (`reference/expectations.md`).

- **If the client asks for a lower price:** say "Let me take that to our Founder" (`reference/objections.md`). Do not name a percentage or suggest one is possible.
- **To raise it internally:** add the question to `reference/question-log.md` and bring it to the daily check-in with the Head of Sales. A deal closing this week is not one of the two cases that justify interrupting someone in Slack (a client waiting on the line, or something sent to a client by mistake).
- **Before anything goes to the client:** the Head of Sales approves every draft first.

One written term applies if this is a Pilot: if the client signs within 30 days of their Discovery Sprint readout, the full $7,500 Sprint price is credited toward the Pilot (`reference/services.md`). That is a standard term, not a discount, so you can state it. Pilot prices are $20,000 or $30,000 as listed there.

If this is a Build, the Founder & CEO prices it, so the price itself is not yours to set or change.
