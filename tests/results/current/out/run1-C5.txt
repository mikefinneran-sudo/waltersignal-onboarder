A good research brief is one page, written before a call, and every line comes from something you can point to. It is in `reference/templates.md`.

**The template**

```
Business:            name, town, what they do, how many people
Fit:                 why it fits (clients.md), or why it may not
Who decides:         the owner-operator's name, if public
Likely champion:     the office manager or equivalent, if public
What they use now:   tools visible on their website or job posts
Why-now signals:     new location, hiring, reviews, a season coming
Three questions:     the first three you will ask, from discovery-questions.md
Sources:             every link you used
```

**A filled example (a practice business, not a client)**

```
Business:            Example Heating & Air (practice only), Springfield, residential HVAC, ~25 people
Fit:                 Good. Growing, hiring, and the job post asks for spreadsheet skills.
Who decides:         The owner, named on the About page
Likely champion:     Office manager, named in a job post for a second dispatcher
What they use now:   Online booking widget on the site; job post asks for "spreadsheet skills"
Why-now signals:     Hiring a second dispatcher; heating season starting
Three questions:     #4 (call to job), #6 (falls through the cracks), #12 (why this quarter)
Sources:             company website, job post, Google Business Profile
```

**What makes it good**

- **Fit is judged against `reference/clients.md`:** 10 to 250 employees, work running on spreadsheets or disconnected tools. If it may not fit, say why.
- **Decider and champion are named** only if the information is public.
- **The three questions are numbered** from `reference/discovery-questions.md` and match what you learned about the business.
- **Every line has a source,** and the sources are listed.
- **Save it in the Vault** in the client's folder (`Clients/<Stage>/<Client>/`) the same day (`reference/second-brain.md`).

**Where you are on day 5:** research brief 2 is today's goal, and it gets reviewed in your first weekly 1:1 with the Head of Sales (Friday, 2:00 to 2:30 Eastern). Brief 3 is tomorrow. The briefs are written on leads the Head of Sales assigns, and from launch you write one before every call.
