A good brief is one page, written before every call (`reference/templates.md`). Every line gets a source link. On day 5, focus on three lines:

- **Which buyer, and why.** One sentence. Example: "1. Owner posts about growth and hiring; this is a workflow question, not a website."
- **Why-now signals.** Hiring, a new location, a season coming.
- **Three questions** you'll ask, picked from `reference/discovery-questions.md`.

The template has the other lines and a full filled example.

Module 5's milestone is three briefs on leads the Head of Sales assigns.

Try it: draft one brief and tell me which buyer you picked and why. Also, which modules are signed off so far, and what have you sold before?
