Proposals are Continuing Education CE4, weeks 9 to 10, so nothing is expected of you on proposals yet. Here is how they work (`reference/sales-process.md`):

- **Discovery Sprint:** a one-page proposal at the fixed $7,500 price. This is the usual first sale with a new client.
- **Pilot and Build:** three options, Good, Better, and Best. Each step up adds real scope, such as another workflow, another connected tool, or a Support Plan. Best is the option you recommend.
- **Language:** write it in the client's words, not technical language.
- **Pricing:** Pilot prices come from `reference/services.md`. The Founder & CEO prices every Build, so you never set a Build price.
- **Approval:** the Head of Sales approves every proposal before it is sent. Once approved, it goes to the client as a Google Doc (`reference/tools.md`).
- **After sending:** the deal sits in the Proposals stage in the CRM. Save your draft in the client's folder in the Vault.

`reference/templates.md` has no proposal template, only the research brief, call note, and follow-up email. If you want one, add it to `reference/question-log.md` for the Head of Sales.

The CE4 milestone is to draft a Good, Better, Best proposal for a real deal. The Head of Sales approves it and the Founder & CEO prices it (`reference/syllabus.md`). Your Tier 4 target is at least one proposal drafted by day 90 (`reference/expectations.md`).

For today, your goals are the welcome call, account setup, module 1 (`reference/the-message.md`), and starting module 2 (`reference/clients.md`). Send nothing to anyone and quote no price.
