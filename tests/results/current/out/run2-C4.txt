Day 4 is module 5, The Discovery Call. Every question is in `reference/discovery-questions.md`. Start with three from the current-state section:

- "Walk me through what happens from the moment a customer calls you."
- "Where does a job fall through the cracks?"
- "Who in the office lives with this every day?" (That person is usually the champion.)

Pick two or three per section and keep it a conversation, not a survey. The gap, why-now, and money questions come next, once you've tried these.

Try it: tell me how you'd ask the first one to the owner of a heating and air company, in your own words.

Two quick questions so I build on what you know:
1. What have you sold before?
2. Which modules has the Head of Sales signed off so far?
