Day 4 is module 5, The Discovery Call (days 3 to 7). The full bank is in `reference/discovery-questions.md`. Start with three:

1. **Why now:** "What made you reach out now?"
2. **Current state:** "Walk me through what happens from the moment a customer calls you."
3. **Gap:** "What does one of those cost you, in time or in a lost job?"

The call runs in this order: current state, gap, solution, next step. Every call ends with a dated next step, logged in the CRM the same day.

Try it: pick a business you know and say how you'd open with question 2. Then tell me in your own words why we don't ask about budget in the first five minutes.

Also, which modules has the Head of Sales signed off so far, and what have you sold before?
