**Solutions Engineering** owns data and security questions (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). The folder has no answer on where client data lives or how it is protected, so don't answer it yourself.

What to do:

- **Tell the prospect:** "Good question. I'll get you a straight answer from our Solutions Engineering lead." (`reference/objections.md`)
- **Log it:** add the question to `reference/question-log.md` for the end-of-day check-in.
- **Send nothing yet:** any written reply to the prospect goes to the Head of Sales first.

The only reason to interrupt is if the prospect is on the line right now. In that case, message the Head of Sales immediately.

Also set a dated next step in the CRM for when you will get back to them.
