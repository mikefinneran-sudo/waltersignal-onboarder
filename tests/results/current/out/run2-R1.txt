**Solutions Engineering** owns data and security questions (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). The security answer itself isn't in my notes, so I won't guess at one. Don't answer it yourself either.

What to do:

1. **Tell the prospect** what `reference/objections.md` suggests: "Good question. I'll get you a straight answer from our Solutions Engineering lead."
2. **Add the question** to `reference/question-log.md` for the daily check-in, with Solutions Engineering as the owner.
3. **Get approval first.** Whatever answer goes back to the prospect, the Head of Sales approves it before you send it.

If the prospect is waiting on the line right now, message the Head of Sales in Slack right away. That is one of the two cases where you may interrupt (`reference/who-owns-what.md`).
