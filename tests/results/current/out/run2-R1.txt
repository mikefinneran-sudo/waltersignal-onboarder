**Solutions Engineering** owns data and security questions (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). The folder has no security answer, so I can't tell you where client data lives or how it is protected.

**What to say to the prospect** (`reference/objections.md`):
- "Good question. I'll get you a straight answer from our Solutions Engineering lead."
- Don't answer the security part yourself, and don't reassure them with anything that isn't written down.

**What to do next:**
1. Add the question to `reference/question-log.md` and bring it to the 4:15 check-in (`reference/meetings.md`).
2. Give the prospect a dated next step so the answer has a deadline, and log it in the CRM the same day.
3. Show the Head of Sales any reply before it goes to the prospect. Everything sent to a client or prospect is approved first.

The only exception is if the prospect is on the line with you right now. Then message the Head of Sales in Slack immediately (`reference/who-owns-what.md`).
