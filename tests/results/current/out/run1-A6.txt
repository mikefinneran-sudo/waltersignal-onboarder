Congratulations. Here is what happens after a yes (`reference/sales-process.md`):

1. **Tell the Head of Sales today**, at the daily check-in (`reference/meetings.md`). If it can't wait for the check-in, that's still a Head of Sales question, but Slack-right-away is reserved for the two urgent cases in `reference/who-owns-what.md`.
2. **Legal & Contracts sends the agreement.** It goes on its own, with nothing else attached. You don't write or change contract wording.
3. **When the signed agreement comes back, move the deal to Closed Won** in the CRM. Finance & Billing then sends the first invoice.
4. **The Delivery Lead runs the kickoff.** You stay the client's contact for anything new they want to buy.

Also do these today:
- Log the call's next step and stage in the CRM, and save the call note in the client's Vault folder (`reference/templates.md`). When the stage changes to Closed Won, move the client's Vault folder with it (`reference/second-brain.md`).
- Send nothing to the client until the Head of Sales approves it, including a thank-you note.

**What the client pays, by phase** (`reference/sales-process.md`):

| Phase | When the client pays |
|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | 100% at signing |
| Pilot | 50% at signing, 50% at go-live |
| Build | 50% at signing, the balance billed monthly over the Build |
| Support Plan | Monthly, starting the month after go-live |

If the client asks about kickoff dates or timelines, don't promise any. Timelines before signing are Solutions Engineering, and everything after signing is the Delivery Lead.
