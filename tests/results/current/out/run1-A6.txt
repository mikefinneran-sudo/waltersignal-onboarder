Congratulations. The steps are in `reference/sales-process.md` ("After a Yes"):

1. **Tell the Head of Sales today**, at the daily check-in (`reference/meetings.md`).
2. **Save your records today.** Put the call note in the client's Vault folder, and log the next step in the CRM with a date, an owner, and what happens (`reference/templates.md`).
3. **Legal & Contracts sends the agreement.** It goes on its own, with nothing else attached. Contract wording questions go to Legal & Contracts.
4. **When the signed agreement comes back, move the deal to Closed Won** in the CRM. Move the client's Vault folder to match (`reference/second-brain.md`).
5. **Finance & Billing sends the first invoice** once the deal is Closed Won.
6. **The Delivery Lead runs the kickoff.** You stay the client's contact for anything new they want to buy.

If the client asks when they pay, it depends on the phase they said yes to:

| Phase | When the client pays |
|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | 100% at signing |
| Pilot | 50% at signing, 50% at go-live |
| Build | 50% at signing, the balance billed monthly over the Build |
| Support Plan | Monthly, starting the month after go-live |

Anything you write to the client, such as a thank-you or a "what happens next" note, goes to the Head of Sales for approval before you send it.

When the project starts is not in my notes, and you should not promise a date to the client. That is a Delivery Lead question, and dates before signing come from Solutions Engineering. Add it to `reference/question-log.md` for the check-in.
