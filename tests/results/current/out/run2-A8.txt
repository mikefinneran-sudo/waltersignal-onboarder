**Quotable (after launch)**

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint (2 weeks) | $7,500 fixed |
| Pilot (6 to 8 weeks), one workflow connected to up to two existing tools | $20,000 |
| Pilot, three or more tools or includes an AI step | $30,000 |
| Support Plan: Essentials | $1,500 per month |
| Support Plan: Standard | $3,000 per month |
| Support Plan: Priority | $5,000 per month |

- **Sprint credit:** if the client signs a Pilot within 30 days of the Sprint readout, the full $7,500 is credited toward it.
- **Support Plans:** they are month to month.

**Internal (qualifying a lead only, never said to a client)**

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Build (3 to 6 months) | Usually $60,000 to $180,000, priced per module from the Pilot. The Founder & CEO prices every Build. |
| Change request | $175 per hour, quoted in writing before work starts. The Delivery Lead sends that quote, not you. |

**Never yours**
- Discounts of any size. You never offer, hint at, or agree to one. They belong to the Founder & CEO.
- Any price not on that page. The Founder & CEO prices it.

If a prospect asks "just send me a price" before you launch, say "Let me get you the right person for that" and bring in the Head of Sales (`reference/objections.md`).
