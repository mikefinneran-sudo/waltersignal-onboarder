Brennan shows all three phases, so it is the story to use when you want to show the whole Research, Validate, Build path. It is in `reference/case-study.md`. Tell it as Brennan's story with Brennan's numbers.

## The story in order

**1. The client.** A 38-person heating, cooling, and plumbing contractor serving three counties. The owner was the decider and the office manager was the champion.

**2. The problem.**
- Quotes took two days, because an estimator rebuilt each one from a spreadsheet price book and old jobs.
- Dispatch ran on a whiteboard.
- Every finished job was typed again into the accounting software.

**3. The three phases.**

| Phase | What happened | Length |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovery Sprint** | Interviews with the owner, office manager, two estimators, and a dispatcher. They ranked quoting first and dispatch second, and said to leave the accounting software alone. | 2 weeks |
| **Pilot** | A quote builder that pulls the price book and past jobs and drafts each quote with an AI step. An estimator approves every quote before it goes out. The agreed number was time from request to quote sent. | 7 weeks |
| **Build** | A dispatch board, a technician phone app for job notes and photos, and an automatic hand-off of finished jobs to the accounting software. Staff were trained by role. | 5 months, in three modules |

**4. The result (Brennan's numbers).**
- Median time to send a quote fell from two business days to four hours during the Pilot.
- After the Build, finished jobs stopped being typed twice.
- Brennan now pays for the Standard Support Plan.

**5. The point to land.** The Sprint said to leave the accounting software alone, and that saved Brennan money. WalterSignal recommends less when less is right. It also ties to the "AI is all hype" objection in `reference/objections.md`.

## Rules for telling it

- Say "Brennan's quotes went from two days to four hours," never "you'll get quotes down to four hours." Never promise a prospect the same result (`reference/the-message.md`).
- Keep the AI step in the story with the estimator approving every quote. Every AI step has a person approving it (`reference/services.md`).
- Leave the dollar amounts out of the telling. The case study lists what Brennan paid, but Build prices are Internal and never said to a client (`reference/services.md`). If you are unsure whether any figure from the case study may be said aloud, add it to `reference/question-log.md` for the Head of Sales at the check-in.
- You are not launched yet, so you quote no price to anyone until the Launch Gate on day 10.
- Anything you send a prospect afterward goes to the Head of Sales first.

## Two-minute shape

1. Who they were and the problem (about 20 seconds).
2. Sprint, Pilot, Build, one sentence each (about 50 seconds).
3. The result, in Brennan's numbers (about 20 seconds).
4. The "leave accounting alone" point (about 15 seconds).
5. A close that ties back to the prospect's own problem (about 15 seconds).

The day 8 goal in `reference/first-ten-days.md` is to tell one case study in two minutes. Say it out loud to the Head of Sales at the check-in, and time yourself.
