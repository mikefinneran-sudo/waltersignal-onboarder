A good brief is one page, written before every call (`reference/templates.md`). Three parts matter most:

- **Which buyer, and why.** Buyer 1 or 2, with one reason.
- **Who decides and who champions.** The owner, and the office manager or equivalent.
- **Why-now signals and your first three questions.** Pick the questions from `reference/discovery-questions.md`.

The example in `templates.md` is Example Heating & Air. It is buyer 1 because the owner is hiring a second dispatcher, so it is a workflow problem, not a website one.

The other fields (tools they use now, sources) are in the template. List every link you used.
