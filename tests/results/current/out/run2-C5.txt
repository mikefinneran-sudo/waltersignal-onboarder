A good brief is one page, written before every call, with a source link for every fact (`reference/templates.md`). Three parts matter most:

- **Which buyer:** 1 or 2, and why. The example says "workflow question, not a website."
- **Why-now signals:** hiring, a new location, a season coming.
- **Three questions:** your first three, picked from `reference/discovery-questions.md`.

The full example is Example Heating & Air in `templates.md`. The other five fields come when you write one.

Try it: draft a brief for one of your three assigned leads and tell me which buyer you chose and why.

Also, which modules has the Head of Sales signed off so far, and what have you sold before?
