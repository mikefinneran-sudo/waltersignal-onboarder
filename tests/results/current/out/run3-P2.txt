Proposals are Continuing Education CE4, weeks 9 to 10 (`reference/syllabus.md`). You are not expected to write one yet. Here is how they work (`reference/sales-process.md`).

**What a proposal is:** the written offer for the next phase.

| Phase | Proposal format |
|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | One page, at the fixed $7,500 price |
| Pilot and Build | Three options: Good, Better, Best |

- **Good, Better, Best:** each step up adds real scope, such as another workflow, another connected tool, or a Support Plan. Best is the option you recommend.
- **Language:** write it in the client's words, not technical language.
- **Pricing:** Pilot prices come from `reference/services.md`. The Founder & CEO prices every Build.
- **Approval:** the Head of Sales approves every proposal before it is sent. That is true of anything that goes to a client, at every tier.
- **CRM:** a deal moves from Leads to Proposals when the proposal is sent.

**The CE4 milestone:** you draft a Good, Better, Best proposal for a real deal. The Head of Sales approves it and the Founder & CEO prices it. There is no proposal template in `reference/templates.md` yet. The Head of Sales will teach the format when you reach CE4.

**Your first day:** the goals are the welcome call, account setup, module 1 (`reference/the-message.md`), and starting module 2 (`reference/clients.md`). You quote no prices and send nothing to anyone until the Launch Gate on day 10.
