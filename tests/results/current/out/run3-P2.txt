Writing proposals is Continuing Education CE4, in weeks 9 to 10, so it is not expected of you on day 1. Here is how it works (`reference/sales-process.md`).

**What a proposal is:** the written offer for the next phase.

| Type | Format | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | One page | Fixed $7,500 |
| Pilot and Build | Three options: Good, Better, Best | Pilot prices come from `reference/services.md`. The Founder & CEO prices every Build. |

- **Good, Better, Best:** each step up adds real scope, such as another workflow, another connected tool, or a Support Plan. Best is the option you recommend.
- **Language:** write it in the client's words, not technical language.
- **Approval:** the Head of Sales approves every proposal before it is sent.
- **CRM:** the deal moves from Leads to Proposals when the proposal is sent.
- **Google Docs:** proposals that go to a client live there, once approved (`reference/tools.md`).
- **CE4 milestone:** you draft a Good, Better, Best proposal for a real deal. The Head of Sales approves it and the Founder & CEO prices it (`reference/syllabus.md`).

A proposal template or layout isn't in my notes. `reference/templates.md` has only the research brief, call note, and follow-up email. Add it to `reference/question-log.md` for the Head of Sales at the daily check-in.

Until the Launch Gate on day 10, you quote no price to anyone, and nothing goes to a client without the Head of Sales approving it first.
