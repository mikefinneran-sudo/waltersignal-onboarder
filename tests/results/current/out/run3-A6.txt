Congratulations. Here is what happens next (`reference/sales-process.md`, "After a Yes"):

1. **Tell the Head of Sales today**, at the daily check-in (4:15 to 4:30 Eastern).
2. **Legal & Contracts sends the agreement.** You do not send it. It goes on its own, with nothing else attached.
3. **Update your records the same day** (`reference/second-brain.md`, `reference/tools.md`):
   - Save the call note in the client's Vault folder.
   - Log the next step in the CRM.
   - When the signed agreement comes back, move the deal to **Closed Won** in the CRM, and move the client's Vault folder to match.
4. **Finance & Billing sends the first invoice** once the signed agreement is back.
5. **The Delivery Lead runs the kickoff.** You stay the client's contact for anything new they want to buy.

What the client pays, and when, depends on the phase they said yes to:

| Phase | When the client pays |
|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | 100% at signing |
| Pilot | 50% at signing, 50% at go-live |
| Build | 50% at signing, the balance billed monthly over the Build |
| Support Plan | Monthly, starting the month after go-live |

Until the signed agreement is back, do not promise the client a start date or a kickoff date. Dates come from Solutions Engineering and the Delivery Lead. If the client asks to change any agreement wording, that is Legal & Contracts. Anything you write to the client in the meantime goes to the Head of Sales for approval first.

Walking a deal from Closed Won to kickoff is Continuing Education CE3 (weeks 7 to 8), so you are not expected to know more than the steps above yet.
