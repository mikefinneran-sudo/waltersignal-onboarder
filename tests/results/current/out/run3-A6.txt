Congratulations. That is in `reference/sales-process.md`, "After a Yes":

1. **Tell the Head of Sales today.** Also log the next step in the CRM the same day.
2. **Legal & Contracts sends the agreement.** It goes alone, with nothing else attached. Send the client nothing yourself without the Head of Sales approving it first.
3. **Finance & Billing sends the deposit invoice**, but only after the signed agreement comes back. Payment is 50% at signing. The deal moves to Closed Won when the signed agreement is returned, not before.

If the client asks when the project starts, that is a Delivery Lead question. Add it to `reference/question-log.md`. Don't promise a date.
