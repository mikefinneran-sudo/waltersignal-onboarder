Congratulations. Here is the sequence (`reference/sales-process.md`, "After a Yes"):

1. **Tell the Head of Sales today**, at the daily check-in (`reference/meetings.md`). Save your call note in the client's Vault folder and log the next step in the CRM the same day (`reference/templates.md`).
2. **Legal & Contracts sends the agreement.** It goes on its own, with nothing else attached. You do not send it.
3. **When the signed agreement comes back, move the deal to Closed Won** in the CRM and move the client's Vault folder to match. Until then the deal stays where it is. Finance & Billing then sends the first invoice.
4. **The Delivery Lead runs the kickoff.** You stay the client's contact for anything new they want to buy.

What the client pays depends on the phase they said yes to:

| Phase | When the client pays |
|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | 100% at signing |
| Pilot | 50% at signing, 50% at go-live |
| Build | 50% at signing, the balance billed monthly over the Build |
| Support Plan | Monthly, starting the month after go-live |

- **Client messages:** anything you write to the client, even a thank-you, goes to the Head of Sales for approval first.
- **Not in my notes:** exactly how Legal & Contracts is told to send the agreement. Ask the Head of Sales at the check-in and add it to `reference/question-log.md`.
- **Held for later:** Support Plans and the full hand-off from Closed Won to kickoff are Continuing Education CE3, weeks 7 to 8 (`reference/syllabus.md`). They are not expected of you yet.
