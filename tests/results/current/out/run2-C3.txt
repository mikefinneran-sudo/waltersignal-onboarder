**How to tell it** (`reference/objections.md`, "Do you have results?"):
- Tell it as that client's result, with that client's numbers.
- Never promise the prospect the same number (`reference/the-message.md`, "Never Say This").
- Point out what each story proves. Brennan's Sprint said to leave their accounting software alone, so we recommend less when less is right. Harbor shows a client can stop after any phase and keep what was delivered.

**Leave out the Build price.** The Brennan table lists a Build price, but Build prices are Internal and never said to a client (`reference/services.md`). The Sprint and Pilot prices are Quotable.

If the prospect wants something in writing, send nothing until the Head of Sales approves the draft.
