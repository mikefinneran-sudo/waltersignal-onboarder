A Build usually takes **3 to 6 months** (`reference/services.md`). The folder has no timeline for a specific company, so I can't narrow that range for a 20-person HVAC business.

- **Why it can't be narrowed yet:** a Build is scoped after the Pilot, in modules, so its length depends on what the Sprint and Pilot find (`reference/services.md`, `reference/objections.md`).
- **Closest example:** Brennan Mechanical, a 38-person HVAC contractor, had a 5-month Build in three modules: dispatch board, technician phone app, and automatic hand-off to accounting (`reference/case-study.md`). Tell it as Brennan's story only. Never present it as what the prospect should expect.
- **What you can say to the prospect:** the Discovery Sprint takes 2 weeks, a Pilot takes 6 to 8 weeks, and a Build is scoped after the Pilot, usually 3 to 6 months (`reference/objections.md`, "How Long Will It Take?").
- **What you can't say:** a delivery date. Dates come from Solutions Engineering after scoping (`reference/the-message.md`).

If the prospect wants a firm timeline, that is a Solutions Engineering question (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). Add it to `reference/question-log.md` for the daily check-in. Anything you send the prospect about timing goes to the Head of Sales for approval first.
