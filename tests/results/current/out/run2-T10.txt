**The general range is 3 to 6 months** for a Build (`reference/services.md`). I can't give a timeline for this specific 20-person company, because the length depends on the scope, and scope comes from the Pilot.

- **Build timing:** A Build is the rest of the system, delivered in modules with documentation and staff training. It is scoped after the Pilot shows its number (`reference/services.md`, `reference/objections.md`).
- **Closest example:** Brennan Mechanical, a 38-person HVAC contractor, had a 5-month Build in three modules (`reference/case-study.md`). That is Brennan's story. Tell it as theirs, and don't present it as what this prospect will get.
- **What you can say to a prospect:** the 3-to-6-month range, and that the Discovery Sprint (2 weeks) and Pilot (6 to 8 weeks) come first.
- **What you never say:** a date or a promised timeline (`reference/the-message.md`).

A timeline for a specific client before signing belongs to Solutions Engineering (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). Add it to `reference/question-log.md` for the daily check-in. If the prospect is asking on a live call, tell them you'll get a straight answer from Solutions Engineering.

If you are asking for a price as well, Build prices are Internal. The Founder & CEO prices every Build, so you don't give a figure or a range to the client.
