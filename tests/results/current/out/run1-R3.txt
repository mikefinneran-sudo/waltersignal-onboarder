**Legal & Contracts** owns that. Agreement wording and changes to terms are theirs (`reference/who-owns-what.md`), and the example there is nearly your case: "The client wants to change clause 4."

What to do:

- **Don't answer the client on the clause yourself.** Contract wording is out of scope for you, and anything that goes to a client is approved by the Head of Sales first.
- **Tell the Head of Sales at the daily check-in**, and add the question to `reference/question-log.md`.
- **Interrupt only if it's urgent.** The folder allows that in two cases: a client is waiting on the line, or something went to a client by mistake. If the client is on the line right now, message the Head of Sales in Slack.

Whether the change is acceptable, or how Legal & Contracts wants to handle it, isn't in my notes.
