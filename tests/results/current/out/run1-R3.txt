That is a Legal & Contracts question. Agreement wording and changes to terms belong to that title (`reference/who-owns-what.md`).

- **Don't promise the client anything** about the change, even a "that should be fine."
- **Log it** in `reference/question-log.md` and bring it to the daily check-in with the Head of Sales.
- **Message the Head of Sales right away** only if the buyer is waiting on the line.

Anything you write back to the client goes to the Head of Sales for approval first.

If the change touches price or a discount, that part is a Founder & CEO question.
