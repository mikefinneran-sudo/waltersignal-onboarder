**The Delivery Lead.** Everything after signing (kickoff, project status, go-live) belongs to that title, and "When does a signed client's project start?" is their example question (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). The Delivery Lead runs the kickoff once the deal is Closed Won (`reference/sales-process.md`).

What to do:

- **Don't give the client a date.** Never promise one. Dates come from the owning title, not from you (`reference/the-message.md`).
- **Tell the client** you are getting the start date from the Delivery Lead. Anything you write to them goes to the Head of Sales for approval first.
- **If the client is waiting on the line right now,** that is one of the two interrupt cases. Message the Head of Sales in Slack right away (`reference/who-owns-what.md`).
- **Otherwise,** add the question to `reference/question-log.md` and bring it to the daily check-in.

You remain the client's contact for anything new they want to buy. Project timing is the Delivery Lead's.
