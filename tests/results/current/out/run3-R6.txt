**The Delivery Lead answers.** Everything after signing belongs to that title: kickoff, project status, and go-live. "When does a signed client's project start?" is the example question listed for it in `reference/who-owns-what.md`.

- **Don't give a date yourself.** Never promise a delivery date (`reference/the-message.md`). The start date isn't in my notes either.
- **Your role now:** once the agreement is signed, the Delivery Lead runs the kickoff. You stay the client's contact for anything new they want to buy (`reference/sales-process.md`).
- **If the client is on the line right now,** that is one of the two urgent cases. Message the Head of Sales in Slack right away (`reference/who-owns-what.md`).
- **If it's a written reply,** send the draft to the Head of Sales for approval first. Anything going to a client is approved before it is sent.
