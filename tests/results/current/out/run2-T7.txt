The folder does say what a logged call must include:

- **Next step:** a date, an owner, and what happens, written in the CRM the same day (`reference/sales-process.md`, `reference/discovery-questions.md`).
- **Stage:** every deal sits in exactly one of Leads, Proposals, Closed Won, or Closed Lost, and you move it the day it changes (`reference/sales-process.md`).
- **Lead source:** log how they heard about us (referral, website, event, or outreach) (`reference/discovery-questions.md`).
- **Closed Lost reason:** log the reason whenever a deal goes there (`reference/sales-process.md`).
- **Call note:** it goes in the client's folder in the Vault the same day, not in the CRM (`reference/templates.md`).
