**Brennan** shows all three phases: Discovery Sprint (2 weeks), Pilot (7 weeks), Build (5 months). The point to make is that the Sprint told them to leave the accounting software alone, which saved them money.

**Harbor** shows that a client can stop after any phase and keep what was delivered (`reference/services.md`).

**Rules for telling it:**
- Never promise the prospect the same result. Say "that was Brennan's number," not "you'd see that."
- Brennan's Build price is in the case-study file, but Build prices are Internal. Never say it to a prospect. Sprint, Pilot, and Support Plan prices are Quotable after launch.
- Don't promise a delivery date (`reference/the-message.md`).
