Win the champion first. The decider is the one who signs. Both are in `reference/clients.md`.

| Role | Who it usually is | What they do in the deal | How to work with them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Decider** | Owner, CEO, or general manager | Signs the agreement and approves the money | Talk about cost, risk, and what they will be able to see and control |
| **Champion** | Usually the operations manager or office manager | Lives with the problem every day and knows where work falls through the cracks | Win them first. They tell you how the work really runs, and the decider listens to them |
| **Users** | Dispatchers, estimators, front-desk staff, warehouse leads | Will use what gets built | Ask to talk to one during discovery. Their answers sharpen the Discovery Sprint |

- **Brennan Mechanical:** the owner was the decider and the office manager was the champion (`reference/case-study.md`). Harbor Dental Group had the operations director as champion and the two dentist-owners as deciders.
- **Discovery questions:** #8, "Who in the office lives with this every day?", finds the champion. #15, "Who besides you weighs in on a decision like this?", finds who else is in the decision (`reference/discovery-questions.md`).
- **Research briefs:** your day 4 brief has a "Who decides" line and a "Likely champion" line. Fill them from public sources like the About page and job posts (`reference/templates.md`).
