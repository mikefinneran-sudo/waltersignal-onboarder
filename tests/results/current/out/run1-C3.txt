**What to point out**
- **Brennan** shows all three phases. The Sprint told them to leave their accounting software alone, which saved them money. WalterSignal recommends less when less is right.
- **Harbor** stopped after the Pilot, kept it running on the Essentials Support Plan, and owns everything built. Each phase stands on its own, and a client can stop after any phase and keep what was delivered (`reference/services.md`).

**Watch these**
- **Build prices are Internal.** Brennan's story lists a Build price, but you never say a Build price to a prospect. The Founder & CEO prices every Build. The Sprint ($7,500) and Pilot ($20,000 or $30,000) prices are Quotable, if the prospect asks.
- **Don't say "you'll get the same result."** Say "that was Brennan's result."
- **Approval comes first.** If you want to send anything in writing to this prospect, the Head of Sales approves it first.
