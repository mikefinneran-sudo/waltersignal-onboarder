**The Delivery Lead answers.** Everything after signing, including kickoff, project status, and go-live, is theirs (`reference/who-owns-what.md`). The Delivery Lead runs the kickoff, and you stay the client's contact for anything new they want to buy (`reference/sales-process.md`).

- **Give no date yourself.** Never promise a delivery date. Dates come from Solutions Engineering after scoping (`reference/the-message.md`). This folder doesn't say when any signed client's project starts.
- **Anything you send is approved first.** If you reply to the client in writing, the Head of Sales approves the draft before it goes.
- **If the client is on the line right now,** that is one of the two urgent cases. Message the Head of Sales in Slack right away (`reference/who-owns-what.md`).
- **If they asked by email or can wait,** tell them the Delivery Lead will confirm the start. Then bring it to the daily check-in.

Also check that the deal is in Closed Won in the CRM, since that is the hand-off to Delivery. Finance & Billing sends the first invoice after that (`reference/sales-process.md`).
