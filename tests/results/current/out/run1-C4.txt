Day 4 is module 5, The Discovery Call. You sit in on the Head of Sales' calls and pass one practice call. Here are three questions to start with (`reference/discovery-questions.md`):

- **Current state:** "Walk me through what happens from the moment a customer calls you."
- **Gap:** "Where does a job fall through the cracks?"
- **Why now:** "What changed that makes this worth fixing this quarter?"

The call runs in this order: current state, gap, solution, next step. Every call ends with a dated next step.

Try it: which of the three would you open with for a restaurant owner, and why?

Two quick things so I build on what you know:
1. What have you sold before?
2. Which modules has the Head of Sales signed off so far?
