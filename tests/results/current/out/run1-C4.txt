Use the question bank in `reference/discovery-questions.md`, in the order of the call from `reference/sales-process.md`: current state, gap, solution, next step. Pick two or three questions per section. It is a conversation, not a survey.

| Section | Questions |
|---|---|
| **Opening: why are we talking?** | 1. "What made you reach out now?"<br>2. "If one part of the business ran better a year from now, which part would it be?"<br>3. "How did you hear about us?" (Log the source in the CRM: referral, website, event, or outreach.) |
| **Current state: how does the work run?** | 4. "Walk me through what happens from the moment a customer calls you."<br>5. "What gets written on paper, or kept in someone's head?"<br>6. "Where does a job fall through the cracks?"<br>7. "Which tools do you pay for today, and which ones does anyone actually open?"<br>8. "Who in the office lives with this every day?" (That is usually the champion.) |
| **Gap: what does it cost?** | 9. "How often does that happen in a month?"<br>10. "What does one of those cost you, in time or in a lost job?"<br>11. "If nothing changes, what does this look like a year from now?" |
| **Why now, why us** | 12. "What changed that makes this worth fixing this quarter?"<br>13. "Have you tried to fix it before? What happened?"<br>14. "What would you need to see to trust a fix?" |
| **Money and decision** | 15. "Who besides you weighs in on a decision like this?"<br>16. "Do you have a range in mind, or would it help to see options?"<br>17. "When would you want this working?" |
| **Next step (every call)** | 18. "Here's what I'll do next, by [date]. Here's what I need from you. Does that work?" |

**Rules for the call**
- Money comes up by the second conversation at the latest. Do not give away free consulting; the call finds the problem, and the Sprint maps the fix.
- Write the next step in the CRM the same day: a date, an owner, and what happens.
- Never ask "Do you want to use AI?" Lead with their work, not the technology.
- Never ask "What's your budget?" in the first five minutes. Earn it with the gap questions first.
- Never ask about competitors' private pricing or the prospect's personal details.
