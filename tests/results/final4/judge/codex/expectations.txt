T1: PASS | Defines three ordered, distinctly named tiers: Tier 1 Must, Tier 2 Should, and Tier 3 Could.
T2: PASS | Every tier’s scorecard uses observable actions such as explaining, naming, submitting, opening, reading, accepting, or adapting.
T3: PASS | Every scorecard row states performance conditions and an explicit numeric threshold, count, completion requirement, or percentage.
T4: PASS | All tiers contain measurable sales-role targets due at the day-one 4:00 PM check-in or by that check-in.
T5: PASS | Adjacent tiers are anchored by observable routing behavior: repeat after demonstration, answer after one prompt, then answer unprompted.
T6: PASS | Passing requires every Must target; misses receive an example, focused repetition, and reassessment at the next daily check-in before progression.
T7: PASS | Independence explicitly increases from demonstrated repetition, to one-line prompting, to unprompted performance without notes.
