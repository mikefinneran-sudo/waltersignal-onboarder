# Objections

Handle objections with confidence. Always stay positive and show the value of what we do.

## "AI Is All Hype."

Tell them AI is the future and that we are experts. Explain our value and our great results. Stay confident.

## "Just Send Me a Price."

Explain that pricing depends on many factors and that we offer great value. Let them know we will follow up soon.

## "Can You Do It Cheaper?"

Remind them that quality matters and that you get what you pay for. Be friendly and keep the conversation going.
