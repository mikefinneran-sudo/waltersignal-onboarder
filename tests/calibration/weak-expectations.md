# Tiered Expectations

## Tier 1: Beginner
Should have a good attitude and be eager to learn.

| Scorecard | Target |
|---|---|
| Shows enthusiasm | High |
| Is improving | Yes |

## Tier 2: Intermediate
Should be getting comfortable with the product and the team.

| Scorecard | Target |
|---|---|
| Good communication | Strong |
| Understands the business | Solid |

## Tier 3: Advanced
Should be a strong performer and a team player.

| Scorecard | Target |
|---|---|
| Hits goals | Good |
| Great culture fit | Excellent |
