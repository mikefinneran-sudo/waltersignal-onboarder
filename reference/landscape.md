# Landscape and Gaps

This file tells you which kinds of alternatives a prospect weighs against WalterSignal and where each leaves the client short, so you can say in your own words where WalterSignal fits. It describes categories, never named companies, and states no competitor prices because the folder holds none.

The descriptions are general and are ideas to understand, not a script. For a specific prospect, ask what they use today (`the-message.md`, "Understand first") before you say anything about it.

## The Four Gaps

Each gap is the opposite of a part of the Core Philosophy (`the-message.md`):

| Gap | Opposite Of | What It Looks Like to the Client |
|---|---|---|
| Hype instead of real solutions | Useful implementations | A promise that does not fix the client's work, or technology for its own sake |
| Hidden or mandatory monthly fees | No mandatory monthly fees; transparency | A recurring bill the client cannot stop, or costs that surface later |
| The vendor owns the code and data | Client-owned applications | The client cannot take what was built to another developer |
| Built without looking at the work | Useful implementations | A build that does not match how the work actually runs |

## The Four Categories

| Category | What the Client Gets | What It Costs Over Time | Where It Falls Short | Where WalterSignal Fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Subscription software vendors** | A product that is the same for every customer | A recurring subscription; prospects may have several already ("We already pay for software") | Hidden or mandatory monthly fees; the vendor holds the software and the client's data in the vendor's system; the work is not mapped first | WalterSignal sells no subscription. It builds for one client, who owns the code, the data, and the accounts, with no per-user or license fees. Support Plans are optional (`what-we-sell.md`) |
| **Freelancers and developers by the hour** | Software built by one person, often to the client's description | Hours billed as they are worked, so the final cost is unknown at the start | Built without looking at the work; one person who knows the system, so a departure leaves the client stuck | WalterSignal maps the work first, sells fixed-price phases so the client knows the cost before it starts, and puts everything in the client's name so another developer can pick it up (`what-we-sell.md`, `clients.md`) |
| **Agencies and large consultancies** | A team and a process | Not in the folder; do not state one | Not in the folder; do not claim a gap. Ask the Head of Sales or Solutions Engineering | Say only what the folder supports: WalterSignal is a 14-person firm that proves one fix on the client's own numbers before the rest is bought |
| **Doing nothing, or spreadsheets and email** | Nothing new to pay for or learn | Staff time: the same job typed twice, quotes that take days, decisions made late (`clients.md`) | No fix; the problem stays and the client cannot see the numbers | WalterSignal starts with a fixed-fee Discovery Sprint that maps the work, then a Pilot that proves one fix before the Build (`what-we-sell.md`) |

## What You Do

- Name each category and its gap in your own words. Different wording that carries the meaning is correct.
- For the agency and consultancy row, say the folder has no detail and name the owner to ask: the Head of Sales, or Solutions Engineering for what can be built (`who-owns-what.md`).
- Never name a real company, never state a competitor's price, and never claim a result for a prospect (`the-message.md`, "Never Say This").
