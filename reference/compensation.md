# Compensation and Goals

This file tells you how a Sales Executive is paid under the standard plan and what the goals are, so you can explain both in your own words. The plan is stated as percentages; it holds no dollar figures. This is the standard plan: the Head of Sales confirms each person's quota and base pay in writing, and your own figures are in that writing, not in this folder.

The Head of Sales owns the plan and leads Block 3 (`who-owns-what.md`). Explain it back in your own words; different wording that carries the meaning is correct.

## How You Get Paid (The Standard Plan)

Monthly pay is base pay plus commission only. There is no monthly bonus: bonuses can be earned only quarterly and annually.

| Part | What It Is | Who Owns It |
|---|---|---|
| **Base pay** | The fixed part of pay. The pay mix is 60% base and 40% variable at target | Head of Sales (the plan); People Operations for base pay, benefits, and time off |
| **Monthly commission** | One flat rate on signed contract value (Pilot, Build, and Support Plan, each as signed). There are no monthly tiers, thresholds, or accelerators. Finance & Billing pays the following month on contracts signed that month | Head of Sales (the rate); Finance & Billing (the payout) |
| **Monthly quota** | Set every month by the Head of Sales on signed contract value (Pilot, Build, and Support Plan). It is the measure for the bonuses, not a threshold for commission | Head of Sales |
| **Quarterly and annual bonuses** | Where meeting and exceeding quota is rewarded; see below | Head of Sales (the plan); Finance & Billing (the payout) |
| **Support Plans** | A signed Support Plan counts toward commission, quota, and bonuses at its signed contract value, because WalterSignal provides the support. Support Plans stay optional: offer one at the end of a Build, never make it a condition, and never pressure a client into one. There are no mandatory monthly fees | Head of Sales |

## Quarterly and Annual Bonuses

A quarter's quota is the sum of its three monthly quotas; the annual quota is the total of the
year's monthly quotas. Attainment is signed contract value (Pilot, Build, and Support Plan, as signed) for the period divided by
that quota. A missed month can be made up within the quarter or year, because bonuses measure the
total.

| Bonus | Attainment | Amount | Paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quarterly** | Below 100% of the quarter's quota | None | Not applicable |
| **Quarterly** | 100% to 124% | 5% of that quarter's base salary | The month after the quarter closes |
| **Quarterly** | 125% or more | 8% of that quarter's base salary | The month after the quarter closes |
| **Annual** | Below 100% of the year's quota | None | Not applicable |
| **Annual** | 100% to 124% | 10% of annual base salary | The month after the year ends |
| **Annual** | 125% or more | 15% of annual base salary | The month after the year ends |

Quarterly examples:

| Attainment of the Quarter's Quota | Quarterly Bonus |
|---|---|
| **90%** | None: the bonus starts at 100%. Monthly commission on each signed contract is still paid |
| **100%** | 5% of that quarter's base salary |
| **130%** | 8% of that quarter's base salary, because 125% or more is reached |

The tier edges are read as stated: 100% to 124% earns the lower amount, and 125% or more earns the higher. If a figure falls between whole percentages, ask the Head of Sales to confirm in Block 3.

What you do: ask the Head of Sales to confirm your quota and base pay in writing, and ask Finance &
Billing about payout questions. Never quote a dollar figure that is not in your written plan.

## Your Goals

Goals are tiered. Day one is the first period; the targets are the scorecard lines in
`expectations.md`.

| Tier | Goal for Day One | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Must** | The eight checks in `expectations.md`, Tier 1 | 8 of 8 at the 4:00 PM check-in |
| **Should** | Four categories and gaps named, three phases named, owning title named for sample questions | 4 of 4, 3 of 3, 3 of 3 |
| **Could** | Not-a-fit requests named, meeting invites accepted, pitch adapted to a business you have sold to | 4 of 4, 100%, 3 of 3 parts |

Quota, set every month by the Head of Sales, is your revenue goal after day one. Other meeting and pipeline targets after day one are not in this folder; ask the Head of Sales.
