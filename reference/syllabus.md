# Syllabus

Training runs in two parts. **Initial Training** (days 1 to 10) ends at the **Launch Gate**. After
launch the new hire is in production, working live deals. **Continuing Education** (days 11 to
90) adds the advanced topics, one at a time.

Every module ends in a milestone. The Head of Sales signs off each milestone at a check-in. A
module is done when it is signed off, not when it is read.

Each module is taught the same way: **tell** (read the reference), **show** (watch the Head of
Sales do it, or read the example), **do** (try it), **check** (say it back, or do it for the Head
of Sales).

## Part 1: Initial Training (Days 1 to 10)

| # | Module | When | Reference | Milestone (signed off by the Head of Sales) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Message | Day 1 | `the-message.md` | Say the one line and the arc from memory. Name three things never to say. |
| 2 | The Buyers | Day 1 | `buyers.md` | Name both buyers, who decides, who champions, and the three qualifying questions. Sort five sample businesses correctly. |
| 3 | The Work | Day 1 | `sales-process.md`, `case-study.md` | Explain Research, Validate, Build in your own words, using the case study. Name the four CRM stages. |
| 4 | Tools and Ownership | Day 2 | `tools.md`, `who-owns-what.md` | All access works. Revenue Operations walks you through the CRM screen; then log a practice call in it. Name the owning title for ten sample questions. |
| 5 | The Discovery Call | Days 3 to 7 | `sales-process.md`, `discovery-questions.md`, `templates.md` | Sit in on every discovery call the Head of Sales runs. Write research briefs on three assigned leads. Pass one practice call with the Head of Sales playing the owner: current state, gap, solution, next step. |
| 6 | Offers, Proof, and Objections | Days 8 to 10 | `offers.md`, `case-study.md`, `objections.md` | Name every public price and where it is shown. Tell the case study in two minutes. Handle five objections in a role-play. |

## The Launch Gate (End of Day 10)

The new hire is launched into production when all of these are true:

1. Modules 1 to 6 are signed off.
2. One live discovery call has been co-run with the Head of Sales, with the next step logged in
   the CRM the same day.
3. The Head of Sales says "launched" at the check-in.

**In production means:** you run live discovery calls with the Head of Sales on the line, keep
your deals in the CRM, quote public prices when asked, and draft follow-ups that the Head of Sales
approves before they go. If the gate is not met by day 10, the missing module is repeated. Nobody
is launched early.

## Part 2: Continuing Education (Days 11 to 90)

One advanced topic about every two weeks, while working live deals.

| # | Topic | When | Reference | Milestone (signed off by the Head of Sales) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CE1 | The Signal Audit in Depth | Weeks 3 to 4 | `offers.md` | Explain to a practice buyer what the audit returns and why it comes before any build. |
| CE2 | The Get-Me-Online Buyer | Weeks 3 to 4 | `buyers.md`, `offers.md` | Handle a practice Dig Drive DIY inquiry start to finish, using only the public prices. |
| CE3 | The Products | Weeks 5 to 6 | `offers.md` | Explain WalterFetch, BananaForce, and BananaBots in one sentence each, and say which buyer each fits. |
| CE4 | Referral Partners | Weeks 7 to 8 | `offers.md`, `who-owns-what.md` | Explain the partner offer and route a partner question to Partnerships. |
| CE5 | Three-Option Proposals | Weeks 9 to 10 | `sales-process.md` | Draft a Good, Better, Best proposal for a real deal, for the Founder & CEO to price. |
| CE6 | Your Own Pipeline | Weeks 11 to 12 | `buyers.md`, `discovery-questions.md` | Source and qualify your own leads with the three questions. Run discovery calls alone. |

## The 90-Day Review

The Head of Sales and the new hire review every milestone, the scorecard in `expectations.md`,
the CRM, and the deals worked.
