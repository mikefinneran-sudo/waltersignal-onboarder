# Syllabus

This file lists every training module with what the new hire must be able to do, how it is
checked, and when. Training runs in two parts. **Initial Training** (days 1 to 10) ends at the
**Launch Gate**. After launch the new hire is in production, working live deals. **Continuing
Education** (days 11 to 90) adds advanced topics, one at a time.

## How Each Module Is Taught

Every module follows I do, we do, you do, then a check:

1. **I do (tell and show).** Read the reference, then watch the Head of Sales do it, or read the
   worked example.
2. **We do.** Do it together with the Head of Sales. The Head of Sales supplies what you miss.
3. **You do.** Do it alone, without notes, with the Head of Sales watching.
4. **Check (the milestone).** The Head of Sales signs off the milestone at a daily check-in. A
   module is done when it is signed off, not when it is read.

## The Mastery Rule

A milestone passes only when every part of it is met. If any part is missed:

1. The Head of Sales names the missed part at the check-in and shows the right way once.
2. You practice only the missed part, then repeat it at the next daily check-in.
3. The module stays unsigned until it passes. Nobody moves on, or is launched, with a missed
   milestone.

## The Launch Gate (End of Day 10)

The new hire is launched into production when all of these are true:

1. Modules 1 to 6 are signed off.
2. One live discovery call has been co-run with the Head of Sales, with the next step logged in
   the CRM the same day.
3. The Head of Sales says "launched" at the check-in.

**In production means:** you run live discovery calls with the Head of Sales on the line, keep
your deals current in the CRM and the Vault, quote Quotable prices when asked, and draft
follow-ups that the Head of Sales approves before they go. If the gate is not met by day 10, the
missing module is repeated under the Mastery Rule. Nobody is launched early.

| CE5 | Your Own Pipeline | By day 84 | `clients.md`, `discovery-questions.md` | Find and qualify five leads with the four questions. Run your first discovery call alone, with a note and a dated next step logged the same day. |

## The 90-Day Review

At day 90 the Head of Sales and the new hire review every milestone, the scorecards in
`expectations.md`, the CRM, and the deals worked.
