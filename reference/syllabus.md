# Syllabus

Training runs in two parts. **Initial Training** (days 1 to 10) ends at the **Launch Gate**. After
launch the new hire is in production, working live deals. **Continuing Education** (days 11 to
90) adds advanced topics, one at a time.

Every module ends in a milestone: a short check the Head of Sales signs off at a daily check-in.
A module is done when it is signed off, not when it is read.

Each module is taught the same way: **tell** (read the reference), **show** (watch the Head of
Sales do it, or read the example), **do** (try it), **check** (say it back, or do it for the Head
of Sales).

## Part 1: Initial Training (Days 1 to 10)

| # | Module | When | Reference | Milestone (signed off by the Head of Sales) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Pitch | Day 1 | `the-message.md` | Say the 30-second pitch with all four parts, from memory. Name three things never to say. |
| 2 | Our Clients | Days 1 to 2 | `clients.md` | Describe a good-fit client, the decider, the champion, and the four qualifying questions. Sort five sample businesses into fit and not a fit. |
| 3 | The Work | Day 2 | `what-we-sell.md` (phases only), `case-study.md` | Explain Research, Validate, Build in your own words, using the Brennan Mechanical story. |
| 4 | Tools, the Second Brain, and Who Owns What | Day 3 | `tools.md`, `second-brain.md`, `who-owns-what.md` | Every account works. Log a practice call in the CRM and save its call note in the Vault. Find one decision in Cairn. Name the owning title for ten sample questions. |
| 5 | The Discovery Call | Days 4 to 7 | `sales-process.md`, `discovery-questions.md`, `templates.md` | Sit in on every discovery call the Head of Sales runs. Write research briefs on three assigned leads. Pass one practice call with the Head of Sales playing the client: current state, gap, solution, next step. |
| 6 | Prices, Proof, and Objections | Days 8 to 10 | `what-we-sell.md`, `case-study.md`, `objections.md` | Name every Quotable and Internal price and say which is which. Tell a case study in two minutes. Handle five objections in a role-play. |

## The Launch Gate (End of Day 10)

The new hire is launched into production when all of these are true:

1. Modules 1 to 6 are signed off.
2. One live discovery call has been co-run with the Head of Sales, with the next step logged in
   the CRM the same day.
3. The Head of Sales says "launched" at the check-in.

**In production means:** you run live discovery calls with the Head of Sales on the line, keep
your deals current in the CRM and the Vault, quote Quotable prices when asked, and draft
follow-ups that the Head of Sales approves before they go. If the gate is not met by day 10, the
missing module is repeated. Nobody is launched early.

## Part 2: Continuing Education (Days 11 to 90)

One advanced topic about every two weeks, while working live deals.

| # | Topic | When | Reference | Milestone (signed off by the Head of Sales) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CE1 | The Discovery Sprint in Depth | Weeks 3 to 4 | `what-we-sell.md`, `case-study.md` | Explain to a practice client what the Sprint delivers, why it comes first, and how the credit works. |
| CE2 | Scoping a Pilot | Weeks 5 to 6 | `what-we-sell.md` | With Solutions Engineering, turn a Sprint finding into a Pilot: one workflow, the tools it connects, and the one number it is measured by. |
| CE3 | Support Plans and the Hand-Off | Weeks 7 to 8 | `what-we-sell.md`, `sales-process.md` | Explain the three Support Plans and change requests, and walk a practice deal from Closed Won to kickoff. |
| CE4 | Writing Proposals | Weeks 9 to 10 | `sales-process.md`, `templates.md` | Draft a Good, Better, Best proposal for a real deal, for the Head of Sales to approve and the Founder & CEO to price. |
| CE5 | Your Own Pipeline | Weeks 11 to 12 | `clients.md`, `discovery-questions.md` | Find and qualify your own leads with the four questions. Run discovery calls alone. |

## The 90-Day Review

At day 90 the Head of Sales and the new hire review every milestone, the scorecards in
`expectations.md`, the CRM, and the deals worked.
