# Syllabus

Training runs in two parts. **Initial Training** (days 1 to 10) ends at the **Launch Gate**. After
launch the new hire is in production, working live deals. **Continuing Education** (days 11 to
90) adds the advanced topics, one at a time.

Every module ends in a milestone. Mike signs off each milestone at a check-in. A module is done
when it is signed off, not when it is read.

Each module is taught the same way: **tell** (read the reference), **show** (see Mike do it or
see the example), **do** (try it), **check** (say it back or do it for Mike).

## Part 1: Initial Training (Days 1 to 10)

| # | Module | When | Reference | Milestone (signed off by Mike) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Message | Day 1 | `the-message.md` | Say the one line and the arc from memory. Name three things never to say. |
| 2 | The Buyers | Day 1 | `buyers.md` | Name both buyers, who decides, who champions, and the three qualifying questions. |
| 3 | The Work | Day 1 | `sales-process.md` | Explain Research, Validate, Build in your own words. Name the four CRM stages. |
| 4 | Tools and Ownership | Day 2 | `tools.md`, `who-owns-what.md` | All access works. Mike walks you through the CRM screen; then log a practice call in it. Say who owns five sample questions Mike gives you. |
| 5 | The Discovery Call | Days 3 to 7 | `sales-process.md` | Sit in on every call Mike runs. Write research briefs on three leads Mike assigns. Pass one practice call with Mike playing the owner: current state, gap, solution, next step. |
| 6 | Offers and Public Prices | Days 8 to 10 | `offers.md` | Name every public price and where it is shown. For any other price, say "Mike will quote that after the call." Say the four ownership terms. |

## The Launch Gate (End of Day 10)

The new hire is launched into production when all of these are true:

1. Modules 1 to 6 are signed off.
2. One live discovery call has been co-run with Mike, with the next step logged in the CRM the
   same day.
3. Mike says "launched" at the check-in.

**In production means:** you run live discovery calls with Mike on the line, keep your deals in
the CRM, quote public prices when asked, and draft follow-ups that Mike approves before they go.
If the gate is not met by day 10, the missing module is repeated. Nobody is launched early.

## Part 2: Continuing Education (Days 11 to 90)

One advanced topic about every two weeks, while working live deals.

| # | Topic | When | Reference | Milestone (signed off by Mike) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CE1 | The Signal Audit in Depth | Weeks 3 to 4 | `offers.md` | Explain to a practice buyer what the audit returns and why it comes before any build. |
| CE2 | The Get-Me-Online Buyer | Weeks 3 to 4 | `buyers.md`, `offers.md` | Handle a practice Dig Drive DIY inquiry start to finish, using only the public prices. |
| CE3 | The Products | Weeks 5 to 6 | `offers.md` | Explain WalterFetch, BananaForce, and BananaBots in one sentence each, and say which buyer each fits. |
| CE4 | Referral Partners | Weeks 7 to 8 | `offers.md` | Explain the partner offer and route a partner question correctly. |
| CE5 | Three-Option Proposals | Weeks 9 to 10 | `sales-process.md` | Draft a Good, Better, Best proposal for a real deal, for Mike to price. |
| CE6 | Your Own Pipeline | Weeks 11 to 12 | `buyers.md`, `sales-process.md` | Source and qualify your own leads with the three questions. Run discovery calls alone. |

## The 90-Day Review

Mike and the new hire review every milestone, the CRM, and the deals worked. Targets for the
review are set by Mike and are not in this folder.

Some advanced topics are taught by Mike directly, such as handling objections and reading a
buyer's numbers. Their material is not in this folder yet.
