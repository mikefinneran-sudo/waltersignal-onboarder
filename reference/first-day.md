# Day One Guide

Day one is small on purpose: modules 1 to 3 of `syllabus.md`, and nothing sent to anyone.

## Before Monday

- WalterSignal is remote-first. Day one runs on video, 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM Eastern (Fort Wayne
  time).
- The Friday before, the Head of Sales sends a welcome email to your personal address with the
  video link and the account invites. If it has not arrived by Friday 5:00 PM Eastern, reply to
  the email your offer came from.
- Have ready: a quiet room, a headset, a notebook, and a computer with a current browser.

## The Day, Hour by Hour

| Time (Eastern) | Block | What happens | Training principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | Welcome | Video call with the Head of Sales: what you have sold before, to whom, and what you want to learn. | Start from what they know |
| 9:30 | Access | Revenue Operations sets up company email, the CRM, the client folders, and Linear. Each one is tested live. | Do, then check |
| 10:30 | Module 1: The Message | Read `the-message.md`. The Head of Sales says the one line, then you say it. | Tell, show, do, check |
| 11:30 | Break | Fifteen minutes. Add any question to your list. | |
| 11:45 | Module 2: The Buyers | Read `buyers.md`. Sort five sample businesses the Head of Sales gives you into buyer 1 or buyer 2. | Three at a time |
| 12:30 | Lunch | One hour, offline. | |
| 1:30 | Module 3: The Work | Read `sales-process.md` and `case-study.md`: one real build, from the first call to live. | Show before do |
| 2:30 | Self-Study | Read waltersignal.io as a buyer would: /consulting, /products, /dig. Ask the onboarder anything. | Learner sets the pace |
| 4:00 | Check-In | With the Head of Sales: say the one line, both buyers, and the three steps. Go through your question list. | Check, then follow up |
| 4:30 | Done | Nothing else today. | |

If the video link fails at 9:00, email the Head of Sales at the address in the welcome email.

## Day One Checklist

1. Access works for company email, the CRM, the client folders, and Linear.
2. You can say the one-line message without notes.
3. You can name both buyers and say why they are never mixed.
4. You can name the three steps: Research, Validate, Build.
5. You know that anything sent to a buyer goes to the Head of Sales first.
6. Your question list is started and was reviewed at the check-in.
