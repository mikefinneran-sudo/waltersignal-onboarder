# The First Day

This file gives the plan for day one in four blocks, what is practiced again during the day, and the
standard that ends it. Day one is a Monday. The day has four two-hour blocks. Each block has one goal. Only
three times are fixed: the 9:00 AM Eastern welcome call, account setup right after it, and the
4:00 PM Eastern check-in (`meetings.md`). Inside a block, work in any order.

Day one follows model, guided, independent, then feedback (I do, we do, you do, then the Head of
Sales checks). Each pass of the pitch below is one step of that sequence.

## Before the Day Starts

- WalterSignal is remote-first, with no office. You work from home on your own internet connection;
  all meetings are on Google Meet and team chat is in Slack. This folder does not state a dress
  code; ask People Operations.
- The Friday before you start, the Head of Sales emails your personal address with the video link
  for the 9:00 AM Eastern welcome call and your account invites. If it has not arrived by Friday
  5:00 PM Eastern, reply to the email your offer came from.
- Have ready: a quiet room, a headset, and a computer with a current browser.

## Your Two Mentors

Each title in `who-owns-what.md` is held by one person. Day one gives you two mentors, the leaders
of two departments: one for the morning blocks and one for the afternoon blocks. Neither is your
manager. The Head of Sales is your manager and runs the welcome call and the 4:00 PM check-in. With
each mentor, you first watch the mentor do the work (model, I do), then you try it with the mentor
guiding you (guided, we do).

| Mentor | Blocks | What They Own (`who-owns-what.md`) | Why You Need It | What You Do With Them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Revenue Operations** leader | 1 and 2 (morning) | The CRM, tool access and logins, access to the Vault and Cairn | What you need to work at all | Watch them open each tool in `tools.md`, then open each one yourself with them guiding. In Block 2, watch them route one sample question to its owning title, then route two yourself with them guiding |
| **Solutions Engineering** leader | 3 and 4 (afternoon) | "Can we build this?", technical scope, timelines before signing, data and security questions | What you need to sell honestly | Watch them answer one sample "Can we build this?" or data question, then say "I will confirm with Solutions Engineering" and name what they would confirm, with them guiding. Then say the pitch with them listening for any claim about what can be built |

The hand-off from the morning mentor to the afternoon mentor happens at the end of Block 2: tell
the Solutions Engineering leader what is open, what is not, and what is on your question list.

## Practiced Again

| Item | First Time | Again |
|---|---|---|
| The 30-second pitch | Block 3, first pass with notes | Block 3, second pass with no notes; Block 4 at the check-in |
| The good-fit client in two sentences | Block 2 | Block 4 at the check-in |
| Never-say items | Block 2 | Block 3, then Block 4 at the check-in |

## Done When

The day is done when the five checks in `expectations.md` (Must) pass at the 4:00 PM check-in:

1. All 8 tools in `tools.md` open on your own computer.
2. Your question list has 5 questions.
3. You describe a good-fit client in two sentences that name size, situation, the decider, and the
   champion.
4. You say the 30-second pitch without notes, with its one idea, the Brennan Mechanical proof, and
   the closing question.
5. You name at least 3 never-say items from `the-message.md`.

If a check is missed, the Head of Sales names it at the check-in and shows one example of the right
behavior. You repeat only that check at the next daily check-in, and the plan does not move on
until it passes.

## Rules for the Day

- You send nothing to a prospect or client, and you quote no price to anyone, including friends and
  family. Anything that goes to a client is approved by the Head of Sales first.
- Anything past day one is not covered here. Say which title owns it (`who-owns-what.md`); the
  Head of Sales tells you at the check-in what day two holds.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- The video link fails at 9:00: email the Head of Sales at the address in the welcome email.
- An account does not work: Revenue Operations.
- You fall behind: say so at the 4:00 PM check-in. The Head of Sales moves the plan.
