# The First Day

The goal of day 1 is small: learn the ground, not the whole map. Modules 1 to 3 of
`syllabus.md`, and nothing sent to anyone.

## The Day in Five Blocks

Start time and place are not in this folder. Mike confirms them before day 1.

| Block | What happens | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Welcome | Mike sets up access to the tools in `tools.md`. Ask what you have sold before. | Start from what they know |
| 2. The message | Learn the one line and the arc (`the-message.md`). Say it back. | Tell, show, do, check |
| 3. The buyers | The two buyers and the three questions (`buyers.md`). | Three at a time |
| 4. The work | Research, Validate, Build, and the CRM stages (`sales-process.md`). | Show before do |
| 5. Check-in | End-of-day check-in with Mike. Say the one line, the two buyers, and the three steps. Go through your question list. | Check, then follow up |

## First-Day Checklist

1. Access works for company email, the CRM, the client folders, and Linear.
2. You can say the one-line message without notes.
3. You can name both buyers and say why they are never mixed.
4. You can name the three steps: Research, Validate, Build.
5. You know that anything sent to a buyer goes to Mike first.
6. Your question list is started and brought to the check-in.

## What Can Wait

Tools in depth, discovery calls, offers and prices, proposals, and the products. They are
modules 4 to 6 and Continuing Education (`syllabus.md`). Knowing what to ignore applies to the first day too.
