# What We Sell

Use this page to name the three phases to a client, to say who quotes each price, and to answer "is that monthly?". Every engagement moves through Research, Validate, and Build, each sold and signed on its own, so a client can stop after any phase and keep what was delivered.

## Who Quotes a Price

No price is hidden from a client. When a client asks about any price, find it in the table below and
say which title quotes it:

| Price | Who quotes it | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint, Pilot, Support Plans | You, after the Head of Sales clears you to quote (past day one) | On day one say "the Head of Sales will confirm the price in writing" |
| Build | The Founder & CEO, per module | Say "the Founder & CEO prices every Build"; never estimate it yourself |
| Change requests | The Delivery Lead, in writing | Say "the Delivery Lead will send you a written quote before any work starts" |
| Anything not on this page | The Founder & CEO | Route it through the Head of Sales |

## The Three Phases

| Phase | What the client gets | How long | Price | Who Quotes It | What You Say |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Discovery Sprint** (Research) | Interviews with the team (one deliverable; the whole Sprint lasts 2 weeks), a map of how the work runs today, a ranked list of what to fix and what to leave alone, a recommended Pilot, and a 60-minute readout with the decider | 2 weeks | **$7,500**, fixed | You, after clearance | "Two weeks to map how your work runs, with a recommended first fix." |
| **Pilot** (Validate) | One workflow (one piece of the work, such as quoting or dispatch) live in the client's business, measured against one number agreed at the start, plus 30 days of support | 6 to 8 weeks | **$20,000** for one workflow connected to up to two existing tools. **$30,000** if it connects to three or more tools or includes an AI step | You, after clearance | "One workflow live in your business, measured on one number you choose." |
| **Build** (Build) | The rest of the system, in modules, with documentation and staff training | 3 to 6 months | Usually **$60,000 to $180,000**, priced per module from the Pilot | The Founder & CEO prices every Build | "The rest of the system, built module by module, with training for your staff." |

**How to say it:** "We work in three phases, each signed on its own: first we map how your work runs, then we prove one fix on your own numbers, then we build the rest." Ask which phase fits where they are today.

**Sprint credit:** if the client signs a Pilot within 30 days of the Sprint readout, the full
$7,500 is credited toward the Pilot. At the readout, say "The $7,500 comes off the Pilot if you sign within 30 days."

**How each price is billed:** the Sprint and the Pilot are one-time fixed prices. A Build is priced
per module, one time. Support Plans are billed monthly. Change requests are billed by the hour. When a client asks "is that monthly?", answer with the billing unit in this paragraph.

## After Go-Live: Support Plans

Every Build ends with an offer of a monthly Support Plan. It is optional: never make it a condition of the Build, and never pressure a client into one.

| Plan | Price per month | What is included | Response time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Essentials** | $1,500 | Hosting, monitoring, and fixes to anything that breaks | 2 business days. Offer it to a client who wants it kept running and needs few changes. |
| **Standard** | $3,000 | Essentials, plus up to 10 hours of changes a month | 1 business day. Offer it to a client who expects regular changes. |
| **Priority** | $5,000 | Essentials, plus up to 25 hours of changes a month | Same business day. Offer it to a client who cannot wait a day. |

Plans are month to month. When a client asks about life after go-live, name all three plans and let the client choose; you can say, "Plans are month to month, so you can change or stop each month."

## Changes Outside the Agreement

Work the client asks for that is not in the signed agreement is a **change request**. It is
quoted in writing at **$175 per hour** before any work starts (the Delivery Lead sends
the quote, not you). When a client asks for extra work, say, "I will have the Delivery Lead send you a written quote before any work starts."

## What Every Client Gets

These are true of every engagement. Say them on every call.

1. **The client owns it:** the code, the data, and every account are in the client's name.
2. **No per-user fees and no license fees.** If a client asks about per-seat or subscription charges for what WalterSignal built, say there are none.
3. **A 90-day warranty** after go-live: anything that does not work as agreed is fixed at no
   charge.
4. **AI steps always have a person approving.** Anything AI drafts (a quote, a reply, a form) is
   checked by the client's staff before it goes out.

## What We Do Not Sell

Marketing websites, IT support, hardware, off-the-shelf software, AI training workshops, and
developers by the hour (`clients.md`). If a prospect asks for one, say so kindly and use the script in `clients.md`.

## Discounts

You never offer, hint at, or agree to a discount. Only the Founder & CEO decides discounts.
