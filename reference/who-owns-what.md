# Who Owns What

Route every question to a **title**, not a name. The titles below are WalterSignal's org chart.
Most answers are in this folder; this page says who owns the rest.

## The Org Chart

```
Founder & CEO
├── Head of Sales ── Sales Executive (you)
├── Revenue Operations
├── Solutions Engineering
├── Delivery Lead
├── Finance & Billing
├── People Operations
├── Legal & Contracts
├── Partnerships
└── Marketing
```

WalterSignal is small. Today the founder, Mike Finneran, holds every title except yours. Ask by
title anyway: "This is a Revenue Operations question." It tells Mike which hat to answer in, and
the folder stays right as each title is hired.

## What Each Title Owns

| Title | Owns | Example questions |
|---|---|---|
| Founder & CEO | Final prices, any discount, the message, strategy, signing agreements | "Can we take 10% off?" "What does the Signal Audit cost?" |
| Head of Sales (your manager) | Your training, milestone sign-offs, the Launch Gate, check-ins, the weekly 1:1 and pipeline review, call coaching, approving every draft before it goes to a buyer, your targets and commission plan | "Did I pass module 2?" "Can I send this follow-up?" "What's my quota?" |
| Revenue Operations | The CRM, its fields and stages, tool access and logins, lead lists | "Which CRM fields are required?" "My login doesn't work." |
| Solutions Engineering | "Can we build this?", technical scope, timelines before signing, data and security questions | "Can it connect to QuickBooks?" "Where does client data live?" |
| Delivery Lead | Everything after signing: kickoff, project status, go-live | "When does a signed client's project start?" |
| Finance & Billing | Invoices, deposits, payment questions, commission and partner payouts | "When is the deposit invoice sent?" "When are commissions paid?" |
| People Operations | Pay, benefits, time off, hours, equipment, workplace policy | "What's the PTO policy?" "Can I expense a headset?" |
| Legal & Contracts | Agreement wording, changes to terms, NDAs, sending the agreement | "The client wants to change clause 4." |
| Partnerships | Referral partners, partner pages, partner questions | "A partner asked about their page." |
| Marketing | The website, inbound leads, content, brand | "There's a typo on /consulting." |

## What You Own

Lead research, discovery notes, the next step on every call, keeping your deals current in the
CRM, and drafts for the Head of Sales to approve.

## How to Ask

- **Most questions:** add them to your question list (`question-log.md`) and bring it to the
  end-of-day check-in with the Head of Sales.
- **Cannot wait:** only two cases. A buyer is waiting on the line, or something went to a buyer by
  mistake. Message the Head of Sales right away.
- The public company email, for buyers, is mike@waltersignal.io.

This folder does not hold anyone's phone number or password.
