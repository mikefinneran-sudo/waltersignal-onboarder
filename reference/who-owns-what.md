# Who Owns What

WalterSignal is small. Most answers are in this folder; most decisions are Mike's. This page says
which is which, so nobody asks Mike something the folder already answers.

## People

| Person | Role | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| Mike Finneran | Founder | Prices, discounts, proposals, contracts, scope, delivery dates, technical questions about what can be built, your pay and schedule, approval of anything sent to a buyer |
| You | Sales Executive | Lead research, discovery notes, the next step on every call, keeping the CRM current, drafts for Mike to approve |
| Referral partners | Outside partners who send buyers | Their own audiences. Never contact a partner's audience directly. Partner questions go to Mike. |

## How to Reach Mike

- **Day to day:** keep a running question list. Bring it to the end-of-day check-in
  (`first-day.md`). Most questions can wait a few hours.
- **Cannot wait:** a buyer is waiting on the line, or something has gone to a client by mistake.
  Interrupt Mike right away.
- The public company email, for buyers, is mike@waltersignal.io.

This folder does not hold anyone's phone number.
