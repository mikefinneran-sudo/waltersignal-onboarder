# Services and Prices

Every engagement moves through three phases: **Research, Validate, Build**. Each phase is sold
and signed on its own, so a client can stop after any phase and keep what was delivered.

Prices on this page are WalterSignal's price list. **Before the Launch Gate on day 10, you quote
no price to anyone** (`syllabus.md`). After launch, you may quote the prices marked **Quotable**.
Prices marked **Internal** are for qualifying a lead; never say them to a client. The Founder &
CEO prices everything that is not on this page.

## The Three Phases

| Phase | What the client gets | How long | Price | Who may say the price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Discovery Sprint** (Research) | Interviews with the team, a map of how the work runs today, a ranked list of what to fix and what to leave alone, a recommended Pilot, and a 60-minute readout with the decider | 2 weeks | **$7,500**, fixed | Quotable |
| **Pilot** (Validate) | One workflow (one piece of the work, such as quoting or dispatch) live in the client's business, measured against one number agreed at the start, plus 30 days of support | 6 to 8 weeks | **$20,000** for one workflow connected to up to two existing tools. **$30,000** if it connects to three or more tools or includes an AI step | Quotable |
| **Build** (Build) | The rest of the system, in modules, with documentation and staff training | 3 to 6 months | Usually **$60,000 to $180,000**, priced per module from the Pilot | Internal. The Founder & CEO prices every Build |

**Sprint credit:** if the client signs a Pilot within 30 days of the Sprint readout, the full
$7,500 is credited toward the Pilot.

## After Go-Live: Support Plans

Every Build ends with an offer of a monthly Support Plan. The client may also take none.

| Plan | Price per month | What is included | Response time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Essentials** | $1,500 | Hosting, monitoring, and fixes to anything that breaks | 2 business days |
| **Standard** | $3,000 | Essentials, plus up to 10 hours of changes a month | 1 business day |
| **Priority** | $5,000 | Essentials, plus up to 25 hours of changes a month | Same business day |

Support Plan prices are Quotable after launch. Plans are month to month.

## Changes Outside the Agreement

Work the client asks for that is not in the signed agreement is a **change request**. It is
quoted in writing at **$175 per hour** before any work starts (Internal: the Delivery Lead sends
the quote, not you).

## What Every Client Gets

These are true of every engagement. Say them often.

1. **The client owns it:** the code, the data, and every account are in the client's name.
2. **No per-user fees and no license fees.** WalterSignal does not charge per seat or by
   subscription for anything it builds.
3. **A 90-day warranty** after go-live: anything that does not work as agreed is fixed at no
   charge.
4. **AI steps always have a person approving.** Anything AI drafts (a quote, a reply, a form) is
   checked by the client's staff before it goes out.

## What We Do Not Sell

Marketing websites, IT support, hardware, off-the-shelf software, AI training workshops, and
developers by the hour (`clients.md`).

## Discounts

You never offer, hint at, or agree to a discount. Only the Founder & CEO decides discounts.
