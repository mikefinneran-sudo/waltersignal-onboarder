# Outbound Campaign Build

This file tells you how to build your first outbound campaign in Block 4: a target list, a message, and a follow-up sequence. You build it and submit it; nothing is sent to anyone on day one. Nothing goes to a prospect until the Head of Sales approves it, and launch happens only after approval (`rules.md`, "Protect the New Hire").

Save the finished draft in the Vault in a campaign folder, and log the sequence steps as dated next steps in the CRM (`tools.md`). Use only the CRM already listed in `tools.md`; no other outreach tool is part of this folder.

## The Three Parts

| Part | What You Build | Where It Comes From | Done When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Target list** | A short list of good-fit prospects | The good-fit description in `clients.md`. Revenue Operations owns lead lists (`who-owns-what.md`); ask them for the list | Every row has the 5 fields below |
| **2. Message** | One short outreach email that names a problem in the prospect's work and asks one question | The Core Philosophy and Block 1 (`the-message.md`), and the gaps in Block 2 (`landscape.md`), in your own words | One idea, one proof, one question; claims only what the folder supports |
| **3. Follow-up sequence** | The steps after the first message, each with a date and an owner | Organized persistence: a next step with a date on every prospect (`the-message.md`, "Company and Product Overview") | Every step has a date, an owner, and an action, logged in the CRM as a draft |

## Part 1: The Target List

Fields for each prospect (use a table or the CRM):

| Field | What to Write |
|---|---|
| Business | Name, town, what they do, how many people |
| Fit | Why it fits `clients.md` (10 to 250 people, work on spreadsheets or tools that do not talk to each other), or why it may not |
| Decider and champion | The owner or general manager, and the operations or office manager, if public |
| Why now | A visible signal: hiring, a new location, a busy season |
| Source | Every link you used |

Do not list a business that matches the "Not a Fit" table in `clients.md`.

## Part 2: The Message

Build it on the Core Philosophy and the gaps in `landscape.md`. Write it in your own words.

```
Subject: [Their process] at [Business]

[Name], [business] [why-now signal], which is usually when jobs get re-keyed twice and quotes slow
down. [One sentence naming the gap from landscape.md that fits this prospect, in your words.]

[One proof from the pitch: Brennan Mechanical, a 38-person contractor, quotes from two days to four
hours after the Pilot.] [One question about their work, for example: How do quotes get from request
to customer at [Business] today?]

[Your name]
WalterSignal
```

Placeholders: [Their process] is the work you noticed; [Business] and [Name] come from your list;
[why-now signal] is from the Why now field. The reply-by date and the next step are in the
sequence below.

What the message must not do (`the-message.md`, "Never Say This"): promise a delivery date, promise
a result for the prospect, mention a discount, lead with technology, or claim a product or
subscription. The Solutions Engineering leader checks that the message claims nothing that cannot be built; ask them
before you submit.

## Part 3: The Follow-Up Sequence

Sales is organized persistence, so every step has a date, an owner, and an action. Enter each as a dated next step in the CRM as a draft, and use real calendar dates you choose.

| Step | Date | Owner | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `[date]` | You | Send the message, once the Head of Sales approves the campaign |
| 2 | `[date]` | You | First follow-up: one new useful fact or question, not "just checking in" |
| 3 | `[date]` | You | Second follow-up |
| 4 | `[date]` | You | Third follow-up. After three dated follow-ups with no reply, the deal is closed lost in the CRM (`tools.md`) with the reason logged |

The folder sets no gap in days between steps. Choose the dates yourself and have the Head of Sales
approve them.

## Submit and Launch

1. Save the draft (list, message, sequence) in the Vault and the steps in the CRM.
2. Ask the Solutions Engineering leader to check the message for claims that cannot be built.
3. Bring it to the 4:00 PM check-in. The Head of Sales gives feedback and approves or does not.
4. Launch only after approval. Nothing is sent on day one.
