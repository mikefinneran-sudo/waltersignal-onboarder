# How a Deal Moves

This file shows how a deal moves from first call to signed agreement: the discovery call, the CRM
stages, proposals, and what happens after a yes.

## The Three Phases

**Research, Validate, Build.** Each phase is sold and signed on its own (`what-we-sell.md`). The
client sees working results at each phase before deciding on the next. Nobody is asked to fund a
large project on the strength of a slide.

Your first sale with any new client is almost always the **Discovery Sprint**. Later phases are
sold from what the Sprint finds.

## The Discovery Call

The first real conversation with a prospect. Its one purpose is to learn how the prospect's work runs; you pitch only in answer to a question, and you agree a dated next step before the call ends. Run it in this order, using the question bank in `discovery-questions.md`:

1. **Current state** (Situation questions). How does the work run today? Who does what, what is on
   paper or in someone's head, and where does work fall through the cracks?
2. **Gap** (Problem and Implication questions). What is not working, and what does that cost them,
   in their own numbers: hours, missed jobs, errors?
3. **Solution** (Need-payoff questions). Ask what fixing it would be worth and what number would
   tell them it is fixed. Then offer the smallest working fix that closes the gap. Usually that is
   "a Discovery Sprint to find out which fix comes first."
4. **Next step.** Every call ends with one, written down: a date, an owner, and what happens.
   The one thing you ask the prospect to do is the next step.

Money comes up by the second conversation at the latest. Do not give away free consulting on the
call: the call finds the problem; the Sprint maps the fix.

## CRM Stages

The CRM is the system where every lead and deal is tracked (`tools.md`). Every deal sits in
exactly one stage. Move it the day it changes.

Every open deal has one owner (you) and one dated next step in the CRM. A deal with no dated next
step is fixed before the weekly pipeline review (`meetings.md`).

| Stage | Enters when | You must do and record | Exits when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Leads** | You are talking to a business and have asked the four questions in `clients.md` | Fill the four answers, the champion and decider, the source, and a next step with a date | A proposal is sent |
| **Proposals** | A written proposal has been sent | Record the proposal date and the three options; follow up on the dates you set | The signed agreement comes back (Closed Won), or the prospect says no or goes quiet (Closed Lost) |
| **Closed Won** | Signed agreement returned | Tell the Head of Sales the same day; move the Vault folder | Delivery takes over (`who-owns-what.md`) |
| **Closed Lost** | Said no, went quiet after three dated follow-ups, chose someone else, or not a fit | Log the reason | The prospect replies with a new need, you move the deal to Leads and log the date and reason |

## Proposals

A proposal is the written offer for the next phase.

- **Discovery Sprint:** a one-page proposal at the fixed $7,500 price.
- **Pilot and Build:** three options, Good, Better, Best. Each step up adds real scope (another
  workflow, another connected tool, a Support Plan). Best is the option you recommend.
- Written in the client's words, not technical language.
- Pilot prices come from `what-we-sell.md`. The Founder & CEO prices every Build. The Head of Sales
  approves every proposal before it is sent.
- Writing proposals yourself is Continuing Education CE4 (`syllabus.md`).

## After a Yes

1. Tell the Head of Sales the same day, at the daily check-in (`meetings.md`). A yes is not one of the two cannot-wait cases in `who-owns-what.md`, so do not use Slack for it.
2. Legal & Contracts sends the agreement. The agreement goes on its own, with nothing else
   attached.
3. When the signed agreement comes back, move the deal to Closed Won. Finance & Billing then
   sends the first invoice.
4. The Delivery Lead runs the kickoff. You stay the client's contact for anything new they want
   to buy.

| Phase | When the client pays |
|---|---|
| Discovery Sprint | 100% at signing |
| Pilot | 50% at signing, 50% at go-live |
| Build | 50% at signing, the balance billed monthly over the Build |
| Support Plan | Monthly, starting the month after go-live |
