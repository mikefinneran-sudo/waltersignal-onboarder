# Glossary

Company words a new hire will hear in the first week, in plain language.

| Term | Means | More in |
|---|---|---|
| **Build** | The third phase: the rest of the system, in modules, over 3 to 6 months | `what-we-sell.md` |
| **Cairn** | The company's searchable memory of decisions and facts, each with evidence | `second-brain.md` |
| **Call note** | The written record of a client call, saved the same day | `templates.md` |
| **Champion** | The person at the client who lives with the problem and wants it fixed, often the operations or office manager | `clients.md` |
| **Change request** | Client work outside the signed agreement, quoted in writing first | `what-we-sell.md` |
| **Check-in** | The daily meeting with the Head of Sales | `meetings.md` |
| **Continuing Education (CE)** | The training topics after launch, days 11 to 90 | `syllabus.md` |
| **CRM** | The system where every lead and deal is tracked (HubSpot) | `tools.md` |
| **Decider** | The person at the client who signs and approves the money | `clients.md` |
| **Discovery call** | The first real conversation with a prospect, to learn how their work runs | `sales-process.md` |
| **Discovery Sprint** | The first phase: two weeks mapping how the client's work runs, $7,500 fixed | `what-we-sell.md` |
| **Internal price** | A price for qualifying a lead; never said to a client | `what-we-sell.md` |
| **Launch Gate** | The check at the end of day 10 that moves you into live selling | `syllabus.md` |
| **Milestone** | The short check at the end of each training module, signed off by the Head of Sales | `syllabus.md` |
| **Module** | One training topic | `syllabus.md` |
| **Pilot** | The second phase: one workflow live and measured, 6 to 8 weeks | `what-we-sell.md` |
| **Pipeline review** | The Monday meeting where the team walks every deal | `meetings.md` |
| **Question log** | The list of questions the onboarder could not answer | `question-log.md` |
| **Quotable price** | A price you may say to a client after launch | `what-we-sell.md` |
| **Research brief** | The one-page prep you write before every call | `templates.md` |
| **Second brain** | The Vault and Cairn together | `second-brain.md` |
| **Stage** | Where a deal stands in the CRM: Leads, Proposals, Closed Won, Closed Lost | `sales-process.md` |
| **Support Plan** | The monthly plan after go-live: Essentials, Standard, or Priority | `what-we-sell.md` |
| **Tier** | What is expected of you at each stage of your first 90 days | `expectations.md` |
| **The Vault** | The company's shared notes in Obsidian | `second-brain.md` |
| **Workflow** | One piece of a client's work from start to finish, such as quoting or scheduling | `what-we-sell.md` |
