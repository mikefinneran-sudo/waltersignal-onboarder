# Tiered Expectations

This file says what is expected of the new Sales Executive at each of four tiers, what is not
expected yet, and the scorecard the Head of Sales checks. Every scorecard line is something the
Head of Sales can see or count: a call heard, a document read, or a CRM record.

Tiers follow crawl, walk, run, which is I do, we do, you do (`syllabus.md`). The Head of Sales
does it, then you do it together, then you do it alone and the Head of Sales reviews. Each tier
asks for more independent work than the one before.

## The Pass Rule

A tier passes when every scorecard line meets its target. If a line misses:

1. The Head of Sales names the missed line at the daily check-in and shows one example of the right
   behavior.
2. You repeat only that line within two workdays.
3. The Head of Sales checks it again at the next daily check-in.

You do not move up a tier on a missed line, and nobody is launched early.

## Tier 1: Day 1 (Learn the Ground, Head of Sales Does It)

**Who works:** the Head of Sales shows; you watch and ask. You say the pitch aloud at the 4:00 PM
check-in.

**Not expected on day 1:** pitching to anyone outside the company, quoting any price, contacting any
prospect, sending anything. (Quoting any price stays off until the Launch Gate on day 10.)

| Scorecard Line | Condition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 checklist items done (`first-ten-days.md`) | By the 4:00 PM check-in on day 1 | 5 of 5 |
| 30-second pitch said from memory | At the check-in, no notes | 3 of 3: one idea, the Brennan proof, the closing question (`the-message.md`) |
| Never-say items named | At the check-in | At least 3 |

**What good looks like:** you say the pitch in your own words and include Brennan Mechanical and
the closing question. **What weak looks like:** you read the pitch, or leave out the Brennan proof or the closing question.

## Tier 2: Days 2 to 10 (Initial Training, Done Together)

**Who works:** the Head of Sales runs each call and you sit in; you then practice with the Head of
Sales playing the client.

**Not expected yet:** running a live call alone, quoting before launch, writing a proposal.

| Scorecard Line | Condition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Modules 1 to 6 signed off (`syllabus.md`) | By end of day 10 | 6 of 6 |
| Discovery calls sat in on | Every call the Head of Sales runs, days 4 to 10 | 100% |
| Research briefs written (`templates.md`) | One per assigned lead, all 8 fields filled | 3 by day 6 |
| Practice call, Head of Sales plays the client | Day 7, no script | All 4 steps (current state, gap, solution, next step) and a dated next step |
| Five objections role-played (`objections.md`) | Day 9, no script | 5 of 5 with all five steps |
| First live call co-run | By day 10, next step logged in the CRM the same day | 1 |

**What good looks like:** on the practice call you ask "how many times a month, and what does one
cost?" and write the answer down. **What weak looks like:** you describe the Pilot before you
know the problem.

## Tier 3: Days 11 to 30 (In Production, Done Together)

**Who works:** you run live discovery calls with the Head of Sales on the line. You quote the
Quotable prices in `what-we-sell.md` when a client asks. You draft follow-ups for approval, and you
present your own deals at the weekly pipeline review (`meetings.md`).

**Not expected yet:** proposals, Internal prices, discounts, scoping a Pilot.

| Scorecard Line | Condition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Calls ending with a next step logged the same day | Every live call, date, owner, action in the CRM | 100% |
| Research brief written before each call | Saved in the Vault | 100% |
| Call notes saved in the Vault | Same day | 100% |
| Anything sent to a client without Head of Sales approval | Any day | 0 |
| Continuing Education CE1 signed off | The Head of Sales watches you complete the CE1 task in `syllabus.md` without notes, with no errors | 1 of 1 by day 30 |

**What good looks like:** a call note with the prospect's own numbers in Gap and a next step with
a date. **What weak looks like:** a call note that says "good call, will follow up."

## Tier 4: Days 31 to 90 (You Do It, Head of Sales Reviews)

**Who works:** you run discovery calls alone; the Head of Sales reviews your notes and your
pipeline each week. You find and qualify your own leads, scope Pilots with Solutions Engineering,
draft three-option proposals, and keep your own deals in the right CRM stage.

**Still not yours:** Build prices, discounts, contract terms, and sending anything that commits
WalterSignal.

| Scorecard Line | Condition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Calls ending with a next step logged the same day | Every call | 100% |
| New leads qualified with all four questions before a second call | Every lead you find (`clients.md`) | 100% |
| Deals in the right CRM stage | At every weekly pipeline review | 100% |
| Proposals drafted for the Founder & CEO to price | Good, Better, Best, approved by the Head of Sales | At least 1 by day 90 |
| First solo discovery call | Alone on the call, with the Head of Sales reading your note after | 1 by day 90, next step logged the same day |
| CE2 to CE5 signed off | Each milestone in `syllabus.md` done without notes in front of the Head of Sales | 4 of 4 by day 90 |

**What good looks like:** you bring a proposal draft in the client's words with the one number it
will be measured by. **What weak looks like:** a proposal that lists features and no number.

Meeting, pipeline, and revenue targets, including your quota, are set by the Head of Sales at launch, not at the welcome call. They are not in
this folder.
