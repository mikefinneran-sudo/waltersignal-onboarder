# Tiered Expectations

Four tiers, tied to the milestones in `syllabus.md`. Each tier says what the new hire should be
able to do, what they are **not** expected to do yet, and the scorecard the Head of Sales checks.
The model is crawl, walk, run: the Head of Sales does it, then you do it together, then you do it
and the Head of Sales reviews.

## Tier 1: Day 1 (Learn the Ground)

**Able to do by end of day:** module 1, and module 2 started. Say the 30-second pitch and
describe a good-fit client.

**Not expected yet:** pitching to anyone outside the company, quoting any price, contacting any
prospect, sending anything.

| Scorecard | Target |
|---|---|
| Day 1 checklist items done (`first-ten-days.md`) | 5 of 5 |

## Tier 2: Days 2 to 10 (Initial Training, Head of Sales Does It)

**Able to do by day 10:** modules 2 to 6, and pass the Launch Gate.

**Not expected yet:** running a live call alone, quoting before launch, writing a proposal.

| Scorecard | Target |
|---|---|
| Modules signed off | 6 of 6 by day 10 |
| Discovery calls sat in on | Every call the Head of Sales runs |
| Research briefs written (`templates.md`) | 3 |
| Practice call passed | 1 |
| First live call co-run | By day 10 |

## Tier 3: Days 11 to 30 (In Production, Done Together)

**Able to do:** run live discovery calls with the Head of Sales on the line. Quote the Quotable
prices in `what-we-sell.md` when a client asks. Draft follow-ups for approval. Present your own
deals at the weekly pipeline review (`meetings.md`). Continuing Education CE1.

**Not expected yet:** proposals, Internal prices, discounts, scoping a Pilot.

| Scorecard | Target |
|---|---|
| Calls ending with a next step logged the same day | 100% |
| Research brief written before each call | 100% |
| Call notes saved in the Vault the same day | 100% |
| Anything sent to a client without approval | 0 |
| CE1 signed off | By day 30 |

## Tier 4: Days 31 to 90 (You Do It, Head of Sales Reviews)

**Able to do by day 90:** Continuing Education CE2 to CE5. Run discovery calls alone, find and
qualify your own leads, scope Pilots with Solutions Engineering, draft three-option proposals,
and manage your own deals through the CRM stages.

**Still not yours:** Build prices, discounts, contract terms, and sending anything that commits
WalterSignal.

| Scorecard | Target |
|---|---|
| Calls ending with a next step logged the same day | 100% |
| New leads qualified with all four questions before a second call | 100% |
| Deals in the right CRM stage at the weekly review | 100% |
| Proposals drafted for the Founder & CEO to price | At least 1 |
| First solo discovery call | By day 90 |
| CE2 to CE5 signed off | By day 90 |

Meeting, pipeline, and revenue targets are set by the Head of Sales at launch. They are not in
this folder.
