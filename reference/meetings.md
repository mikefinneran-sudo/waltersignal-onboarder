# Meetings

This file lists the meetings on day one, what each is for, and who runs it. The Head of Sales sends
the calendar invites; accept each one the day it arrives. Meetings that start after day one are
out of scope here; the Head of Sales tells you about them at the check-in.

| Meeting | Purpose | Owner | When (Eastern) | Who | What You Do |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome Call | Meet and set goals | Head of Sales | Day 1, 9:00 AM | You and the Head of Sales | Say what you have sold before and what you want to learn |
| Account Setup | Every tool works | Revenue Operations | Day 1, after the welcome call | You and Revenue Operations | Open every tool and say which does not work |
| Daily Check-In | Questions, checks against `expectations.md`, tomorrow's first task | Head of Sales | Every workday, 4:00 to 4:30 PM; first one is day 1 | You and the Head of Sales | Bring your question list |

## Daily Check-In

1. Read out your question list from `question-log.md`, one question at a time.
2. Say the pitch, describe the good-fit client, and name the never-say items (`expectations.md`).
3. Write down the Head of Sales's first task for tomorrow and say it back.
