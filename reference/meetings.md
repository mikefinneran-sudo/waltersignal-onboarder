# Meetings

This file lists every meeting, what each is for, and who runs it. Each one starts on a set day, so day one has only the first check-in. The Head of Sales sends the calendar invites; accept each one the day it arrives.

| Meeting | Purpose | Owner | When (Eastern) | Starts | Who | What You Do |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily Check-In | Questions, sign-offs, next day's first task | Head of Sales | Every workday, 4:00 to 4:30 through day 10; 4:15 to 4:30 after launch | Day 1 | You and the Head of Sales | Bring your question list |
| Weekly 1:1 | Your scorecard and coaching | Head of Sales | Fridays, 2:00 to 2:30 | Day 5 (first Friday) | You and the Head of Sales | Bring four items: one win, one hard thing, your scorecard, and anything not on your question list |
| Weekly Pipeline Review | Walk every deal and its next step | Head of Sales | Mondays, 10:00 to 10:45 | Day 6 (second Monday) | Head of Sales (runs it), you, Revenue Operations, Solutions Engineering, Delivery Lead | Listen on days 6 to 10, then present your own deals |

## One-Time Meetings

| Meeting | Purpose | Owner | When (Eastern) | Who | What You Do |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome Call | Meet and set goals | Head of Sales | Day 1, 9:00 AM | You and the Head of Sales | Say what you have sold before and what you want to learn |
| Account Setup | Every tool works | Revenue Operations | Day 1, after the welcome call | You and Revenue Operations | Open every tool and say which does not work |
| Launch Gate Check | Decide launch into production | Head of Sales | Day 10, at the daily check-in | You and the Head of Sales (`syllabus.md`) | Say the pitch and handle five objections |

## Daily Check-In

1. Read out your question list from `question-log.md`, one question at a time.
2. If a module is ready, ask the Head of Sales to watch you do its task and sign it off (`syllabus.md`).
3. Write down the Head of Sales's first task for tomorrow and say it back.

## Weekly 1:1

Your meeting, not a status report. You talk first. The agenda has four items, listed below.

| Minutes | Item | You bring |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | One win and one thing that was hard this week | Both, in a sentence each |
| 5 to 15 | Your scorecard for your tier in `expectations.md` | Your numbers against each target |
| 15 to 25 | One skill to practice next week, taught tell, show, do, check | A call or draft you want coached |
| 25 to 30 | Anything not on the question list | Whatever is on your mind |

## Weekly Pipeline Review

The team walks the CRM, deal by deal, in stage order (`sales-process.md`).

- **Days 6 to 10:** you listen and take notes. You do not present.
- **From launch:** you present your own deals. For each: the stage, the next step, and its date.
- **Rule:** a deal with no next step logged in the CRM is fixed before the meeting, not in it.

## The 90-Day Review

One meeting at day 90 with the Head of Sales, described in `syllabus.md`. Bring your scorecards from `expectations.md` and a list of the deals you worked.
