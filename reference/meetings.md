# Meetings

Three recurring meetings, all on video and all owned by the Head of Sales. Each one starts on a
set day, so day one has only the first check-in. The Head of Sales sends the calendar invites.

| Meeting | When (Eastern) | Starts | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily Check-In | Every workday, 4:00 to 4:30 through day 10; 4:15 to 4:30 after launch | Day 1 | You and the Head of Sales |
| Weekly 1:1 | Fridays, 2:00 to 2:30 | Day 5 (first Friday) | You and the Head of Sales |
| Weekly Pipeline Review | Mondays, 10:00 to 10:45 | Day 6 (second Monday) | Head of Sales (runs it), you, Revenue Operations, Solutions Engineering, Delivery Lead |

## Daily Check-In

1. Your question list from `question-log.md`, one question at a time.
2. Milestone sign-offs, if a module is ready (`syllabus.md`).
3. The one thing you do first tomorrow.

## Weekly 1:1

Your meeting, not a status report. You talk first.

| Minutes | Item | You bring |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | One win and one thing that was hard this week | Both, in a sentence each |
| 5 to 15 | Your scorecard for your tier in `expectations.md` | Your numbers against each target |
| 15 to 25 | One skill to practice next week, taught tell, show, do, check | A call or draft you want coached |
| 25 to 30 | Anything not on the question list | Whatever is on your mind |

## Weekly Pipeline Review

The team walks the CRM, deal by deal, in stage order (`sales-process.md`).

- **Days 6 to 10:** you listen and take notes. You do not present.
- **From launch:** you present your own deals. For each: the stage, the next step, and its date.
- **Rule:** a deal with no next step logged in the CRM is fixed before the meeting, not in it.

## The 90-Day Review

One meeting at day 90 with the Head of Sales, described in `syllabus.md`.
