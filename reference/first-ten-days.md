# The First Ten Days

This file gives the goals for each of the first ten days, what is practiced again, and the
standard that ends the day. Each day has goals, not a schedule. Work through the day's goals in
any order; the day is done when its "done when" line is true. The only fixed times are the
meetings in `meetings.md`. Day 1 is a Monday, so day 5 is Friday and day 6 is the next Monday.

Each day's goals come from one module (`syllabus.md`). A module is one topic with a milestone: a
check the Head of Sales signs off at the daily check-in. Each module moves from the Head of Sales
doing it, to doing it together, to you doing it alone. If a "done when" line is missed, repeat
only the missed part and show it again at the next check-in (the Mastery Rule in `syllabus.md`).

## Before Day 1

- WalterSignal is remote-first, with no office. You work from home on your own internet
  connection; all meetings are on Google Meet and team chat is in Slack.
- The Friday before you start, the Head of Sales emails your personal address with the video link
  for the 9:00 AM Eastern welcome call on day 1 and your account invites. If it has not arrived by
  Friday 5:00 PM Eastern, reply to the email your offer came from.
- Have ready: a quiet room, a headset, and a computer with a current browser.

## Day 1 Checklist

1. Every account in `tools.md` works.
2. You can say the 30-second pitch without notes.
3. You can describe a good-fit client, and the decider and champion inside one.
4. You know that anything sent to a client goes to the Head of Sales first.
5. Your question list is started and was reviewed at the check-in.

## If Something Goes Wrong

- The video link fails at 9:00 on day 1: email the Head of Sales at the address in the welcome
  email.
- An account does not work: Revenue Operations.
- You fall behind a day's goals: say so at the daily check-in. The Head of Sales moves the plan;
  nobody is launched early or skips a milestone.
